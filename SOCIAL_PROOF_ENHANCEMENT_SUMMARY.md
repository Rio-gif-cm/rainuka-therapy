### 1. Research Conducted
Reviewed 2-3 credible sources on testimonial psychology:

1. **Trustmary (2025)** — "Why Testimonials Convert Customers Faster"

2. **Testimonial.to (2025)** — "Unlock Social Proof Psychology for Conversions"

   - Credibility dimensions: expertise, trustworthiness, contextual understanding

   - Functional outcomes: return to work/school, relationships, re-engagement
   - Concrete metrics beyond symptom reduction

- ✓ Three trust signals matter: expertise, trustworthiness, contextual fit
