# Couples & Relationship Therapy Page - Deployment Summary

## Overview
Created a comprehensive couples and relationship therapy landing page (`/couples`) with research-backed content on relationship issues, communication patterns, conflict resolution, infidelity recovery, and practical guidance.

## Key Sections

### 1. **Communication Patterns** (Research-Based)
- Gottman's Four Horsemen analysis (criticism, contempt, defensiveness, stonewalling)
- Antidotes for each communication pattern
- Balanced communication framework (equality-based conflict resolution)

### 2. **Conflict Styles**
- Pursuer-Withdrawer cycle (most common dynamic)
- Blamer and Placater styles
- How to break destructive patterns
- Win-win vs. win-lose solutions

### 3. **Infidelity & Betrayal Recovery** (Gottman Trust Revival Method)
- Phase 1: Atonement (1-3 months) - taking responsibility, answering questions
- Phase 2: Attunement (3-12 months) - understanding root causes, ATTUNE model
- Phase 3: Attachment (6-24 months) - rebuilding trust through consistent action
- Recovery timeline: 1-2 years of active therapy
- Success factors and red flags

### 4. **Reconnection Approaches**
- Love Maps (Gottman) - deep knowledge of partner's inner world
- Turning Toward Bids - responding to connection attempts
- Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) - 70% couples become symptom-free
- Repair Attempts - strongest predictor of relationship recovery
- Shared Meaning - reconnecting over core values

### 5. **Comprehensive FAQ**
- What couples therapy looks like (structure, frequency)
- Timeline expectations (8 weeks to 2 years depending on issues)
- Single-partner therapy vs. couples work
- Cost and insurance coverage (sliding scale available)
- Confidentiality and privacy protocols
- How secrets/infidelity are handled
- Safety concerns (abuse, violence)
- Reframing therapy as commitment, not failure

### 6. **Couple Testimonial**
*"Sarah & Michael, 12 years married"* — Story of reconnection after 12 years of disconnection, learning to communicate, breaking pursuer-withdrawer pattern.
