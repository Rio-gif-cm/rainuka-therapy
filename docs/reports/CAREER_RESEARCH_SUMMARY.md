### 2. **Copy Enhancement: App/Career/Page.tsx** ✅

**Improvement Type:** Anti-perfectionism messaging (highest-impact option)

**Original (Line 157-160):**
```
Perfectionism & Overwork: Understanding what perfectionism costs you. 
Building permission to be "good enough."
```

**Enhanced (Research-Backed):**
```
Perfectionism & Overwork: Not everything requires perfection to be valuable—but 
high-achievers often learned it does. We separate your standards (worth keeping) 
from the fear underneath them (worth releasing). Your ambition doesn't need anxiety 
as its fuel.
```

**Psychological Reasoning:**
- **Names the pattern:** Distinguishes perfectionism (fear-driven) from high standards (valuable)
- **Validates achievement:** "Your standards are worth keeping" (prevents clients thinking therapy means lowering bar)
- **Offers permission:** "Anxiety is removable" (separates identity from outcomes)
- **Addresses the blocker:** "Ambition doesn't need anxiety" (responds to "won't I lose my edge?" fear)
- **Research-backed:** Grounded in meta-analyses showing perfectionistic *concerns* undermine performance while high standards drive it
