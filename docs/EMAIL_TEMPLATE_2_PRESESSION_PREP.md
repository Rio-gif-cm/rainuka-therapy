# Email Template 2: Pre-Session Prep Email

## Subject Line
**Recommended:** `Ready for Tuesday? Here's what to expect`

**Why this works:** References specific day, validates anticipatory emotion ("Ready for?"), promises practical info ("Here's what to expect")

**Alternatives:**
- `What to expect on [DATE] at [TIME]`
- `[NAME], your session is [X] hours away — you've got this`
- `Pre-session tips for [DATE]`

---

## Send Timing
- **For first-time clients:** 48 hours before session
- **For established clients:** 24 hours before session
- **Trigger:** Automated based on appointment datetime

---

## Template Structure (4 Sections)

### Standard Version (Multi-Persona)

```
Hi [CLIENT_FIRST_NAME],

Your session is [X] hours away on [DAY], [DATE] at [TIME]. Let me tell you what to expect, and a few things that might help you feel ready.

---

## What Happens During the Session

**First 5-10 minutes:** We'll settle in. If you haven't filled out the intake form yet, we'll do that now (no stress if you forgot—it's fine).

**Middle 40-50 minutes:** We'll talk about what brought you here. I'll ask questions to understand your situation. You'll do most of the talking—this is your time.

**Last 5 minutes:** We'll recap what we talked about and set some direction for next time.

**The whole session:** 50 minutes of confidential, judgment-free space. Anything you share stays between us.

---

## Common Feelings (Totally Normal)

- **Nervous?** That's the anticipatory anxiety talking. It goes away about 10 minutes in.
- **Worried you'll cry?** Many people do. Tissues are here. It's OK. Crying often means you're in the right place.
- **Afraid you'll forget what you wanted to say?** Bring notes. Seriously, I see clients refer to notes all the time.
- **Not sure what to say first?** Start with "I'm not sure where to start." That's a perfect opening.

---

## How to Prepare (Optional, But Helpful)

1. **Get enough sleep the night before.** Your brain works better, and you'll be less reactive.
2. **Eat something before the session.** Low blood sugar makes anxiety worse.
3. **Bring water.** Talking about emotions dehydrates you. Sip water, it helps.
4. **Think about what brought you here.** Not obsessively—just jot down a few bullet points if it helps: "Postpartum anxiety," "Career burnout," "ADHD diagnosis," etc.
5. **Silence your phone.** 50 minutes of uninterrupted space is rare. Take it.

---

## See You [DATE] at [TIME]

I'm looking forward to meeting you. You're taking a big step by being here.

If anything comes up and you need to reschedule, click here: [RESCHEDULE_LINK]
If you have urgent questions, reply to this email or call [PHONE].

Let's do this.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Customization by Persona

### Perinatal Version

**Subject line variation:** `[NAME], you're showing up for yourself — I see that`

**"What Happens During the Session" (modified):**

```
## What Happens During the Session

**First 5-10 minutes:** We'll settle in. I'll make tea or offer water. If this is your first time, we'll talk about how therapy works and what's confidential.

**Middle 40-50 minutes:** You'll tell me what's been going on. For parents in the postpartum period, this might be: anxiety, intrusive thoughts, identity loss, overwhelm, or just feeling disconnected. Whatever it is, I've heard it before.

**Last 5 minutes:** We'll talk about what resonated and what we might focus on next time.

**The whole session:** A space where you can be honest about motherhood, postpartum, and everything that comes with it. No judgment.
```

**"Common Feelings" (modified):**

```
## Common Feelings You Might Have

- **Nervous or guilty for "needing" therapy?** Stop. Postpartum mental health is real. Getting help is an act of self-love.
- **Worried you'll cry or break down?** You probably will, and that's exactly why you're here. I have tissues, and I'm trained for this.
- **Afraid you'll be judged for intrusive thoughts?** No judgment here. Intrusive thoughts are common in postpartum anxiety, and we know how to work with them.
- **Overwhelmed because you "should" be happy"?** Motherhood is complex. You can love your baby AND struggle. Both things are true.
```

**"How to Prepare" (modified):**

```
## How to Prepare (You've Earned This Time)

1. **Get help with the baby if you can.** Even 50 minutes of uninterrupted adult conversation is a gift. Take it.
2. **Eat something before the session.** Postpartum bodies need fuel. Blood sugar drops = anxiety spikes.
3. **Bring water and a snack.** I have both here, but having your own is nice.
4. **Dress for comfort.** You might leak (that's why I have tissues). Wear what feels good.
5. **Tell your partner/support person:** "I'm going to therapy Thursday. I need this time." You don't need permission, but saying it out loud helps.
```

### ADHD Version

**Subject line variation:** `[NAME], Tuesday at [TIME] — no prep required, just you`

**"What Happens During the Session" (modified):**

```
## What Happens During the Session

**First 5-10 minutes:** We'll settle in. If ADHD gives you the fidgets, that's cool—you can move, doodle, or just sit.

**Middle 40-50 minutes:** You'll tell me what's going on. ADHD, executive function, perfectionism, shame, late diagnosis, whatever. I'm trained in neurodivergence, and you're not "broken."

**Last 5 minutes:** We'll recap and set a goal for next time.

**The whole session:** A judgment-free zone where your brain—the way it actually works—is understood.
```

**"Common Feelings" (modified):**

```
## Common Feelings (They're All Legit)

- **Worried you'll hyperfocus on something irrelevant?** That's ADHD. We'll keep you on track gently. No shame.
- **Afraid you'll forget what you wanted to say?** Bring a list, voice memo, or just say "I had something, but I forgot." All normal.
- **Concerned you'll come across as scattered?** That's literally what ADHD looks like. I get it.
- **Feeling shame about lateness/disorganization?** That's trauma talking, not a character flaw. Let's work through it.
```

**"How to Prepare" (modified):**

```
## How to Prepare (Keep It Simple)

1. **Set a phone reminder for 15 minutes before.** ADHD time blindness is real.
2. **Eat before you come.** Hungry = more scattered.
3. **Bring water.** Dehydration worsens executive function.
4. **No prep required.** Seriously. Just show up.
5. **Jot down 1-2 things if you think you'll forget.** That's it.
```

### Career Coaching Version

**Subject line variation:** `[NAME], let's talk about what comes next`

**"What Happens During the Session" (modified):**

```
## What Happens During the Session

**First 5-10 minutes:** We'll settle in. I'll ask what brought you in—burnout, career transition, identity loss, perfectionism, all of it.

**Middle 40-50 minutes:** We'll explore what happened, what's true about you, and what direction feels right. This is exploratory, not prescriptive.

**Last 5 minutes:** We'll identify what resonated and what we might dig into next time.

**The whole session:** A confidential space to think out loud about your career and life.
```

**"Common Feelings" (modified):**

```
## Common Feelings (They're Valid)

- **Burned out and guilty for wanting to quit?** Burnout is data. It's telling you something needs to change.
- **Scared you've "failed" at your career?** Career isn't linear. Pivoting is growth, not failure.
- **Worried you're too old/too late to change?** Career transitions happen at any age. Let's figure out yours.
- **Uncertain what you actually want?** That's the work we do. No answer yet is fine.
```

**"How to Prepare" (modified):**

```
## How to Prepare (Optional Reflection)

1. **Think about: What does success look like in 1 year? 5 years?** Don't overthink—just gut reactions.
2. **Reflect on: What energizes you vs. drains you at work?** Even one example helps.
3. **Bring any notes, job postings, or ideas.** Visual helps, but optional.
4. **Show up authentically.** The more honest you are, the better we can work together.
```

Track: which subject line gets highest open rate? Use that for future sends.


### Personalization Logic
```
IF client.primary_reason.includes("postpartum") OR client.primary_reason.includes("perinatal")
  USE perinatal_version
ELSE IF client.primary_reason.includes("ADHD") OR client.diagnosed_with.includes("ADHD")
  USE adhd_version
ELSE IF client.primary_reason.includes("career") OR client.session_type == "career_coaching"
  USE career_version
ELSE
  USE standard_version
END
```
