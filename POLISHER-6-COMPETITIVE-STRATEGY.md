## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**The Core Problem:** Rainuka operates in a crowded therapy market but is **completely invisible** to the mechanisms that drive 2026 discovery:
1. **AI search** (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) — 0/96 citations
2. **Keyword rankings** on local + specialty keywords — ranked on 0 target keywords
3. **Backlink authority** — 0-5 backlinks vs. competitors' 200-500+
4. **Content depth per specialty** — 1-2 pages vs. competitors' 15-30+ pages

**Rainuka's Only Current Edge:** Website speed (1.8s LCP vs. competitors' 2-3s). But speed ranks zero keywords.

**The Opportunity:** Rainuka's specialization is **underserved.** The intersection of **ADHD + Perinatal + Career for high-achieving women** has nearly zero direct competitors in the Bay Area. This is a winnable niche if we build authority around it.

## PART 2: COMPETITIVE POSITIONING STRATEGY

### Current Problem: Generic Positioning
**Current:** "Trauma-informed therapist for ADHD, perinatal, and career"
- **Issues:** 500+ therapists in Bay Area claim this exact position
- **Result:** Zero differentiation, zero keyword rankings, zero AI search mentions

### Winning Position: Niche Authority
**Proposed:** "ADHD diagnosis specialist for high-achieving women in perinatal transition"

#### Why This Wins:
1. **Specificity**: ~5-10 therapists in Bay Area vs. 500 generalists
2. **High commercial intent**: Women actively searching for this exact help
3. **Underserved keywords**: "ADHD postpartum diagnosis," "late ADHD diagnosis motherhood," "busy professional ADHD women"
4. **Backlink opportunities**: ADHD women's networks, perinatal communities, career blogs (niche-specific)
5. **AI search fit**: Specific niches rank better than broad services in AI citations

#### Three Unique Angles for Content:
1. **ADHD + Perinatal Intersection** (rare niche)
   - "Why ADHD diagnosis often comes during motherhood"
   - "Postpartum ADHD diagnosis: The double diagnosis moment"
   - "ADHD + perinatal anxiety: Compounding factors"

2. **ADHD + Career** (high-earning professional focus)
   - "ADHD diagnosis as a career inflection point"
   - "Late ADHD diagnosis and career burnout recovery"
   - "Executive function coaching + therapy for career transitions"

3. **Postpartum ADHD Diagnosis** (educational positioning)
   - "Why ADHD emerges postpartum (and what to do)"
   - "ADHD diagnosis + motherhood identity reconstruction"
   - Partner with postpartum support organizations

## PART 4: COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION FRAMEWORK

### Rainuka's Unique Positioning vs. Competitors

| Competitor Type | Their Strength | Our Differentiation | Our Competitive Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Psychology Today** | Massive scale (500K therapists) | Individual specialization | Personal relationship, tailored expertise |
| **BetterHelp** | 7K+ therapists, insurance integration | Niche focus (ADHD + perinatal + career) | Specialist depth, not generalist breadth |
| **Zencare** | Platform play, values-driven | Individual therapist, transparent practices | Direct relationship, no platform friction |
| **Local generalists** | Established, many reviews | Specific niche authority (ADHD + perinatal) | Better search ranking for exact fit customers |

### Content Moats to Build (Defensible Advantages)

1. **"ADHD + Motherhood" content library** (currently undefended)
   - Blog posts, videos, downloadables on this specific intersection
   - Competitors cover ADHD OR motherhood, not the combo
   - Rainuka becomes "the go-to therapist for ADHD + pregnancy transition"

2. **Perinatal + Neurodiversity integration** (Rainuka's genuine expertise)
   - Postpartum ADHD diagnosis, postpartum autism recognition, perinatal neurodiversity
   - Very few therapists specialize in both
   - Position as thought leader in the underserved niche

3. **Career + Therapy integration** (rare in therapy space)
   - Career burnout, job transition, professional identity + mental health
   - Career counselors don't talk about therapy; therapists don't talk about career strategy
   - Rainuka bridges this gap
