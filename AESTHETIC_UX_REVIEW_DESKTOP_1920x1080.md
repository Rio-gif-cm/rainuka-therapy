   - Success states should feel celebratory
   - Impact: Improves booking conversion confidence

2. **Image Optimization**
   - Ensure therapist bio photos are high-quality (professional but warm)
   - Consider adding subtle shadow or border
   - Impact: Builds trust and approachability

3. **Micro-interaction Polish**
   - Add subtle hover effects to CTAs (scale + shadow)
   - Form inputs should have focus ring in burgundy-600
   - Impact: Feels more polished; signals interactivity

### Medium Priority:

4. **Section Transitions**: Alternate background colors between sections for visual rhythm
5. **Accent Color Distribution**: Use sea-blue more for "research-backed" callouts
6. **Social Proof**: Add testimonial cards with blush background and rating indicators

### Low Priority:
