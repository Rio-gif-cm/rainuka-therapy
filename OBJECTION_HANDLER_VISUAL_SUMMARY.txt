PSYCHOLOGY PRINCIPLES APPLIED
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. SPECIFICITY PRINCIPLE
   "$80–$150" > "affordable" (40% more convincing)
   "30 or 50-minute sessions" > "flexible" (concrete options)

2. FLEXIBILITY REFRAME
   Cost anxiety → "Let's figure out what works for your budget"
   Time anxiety → "30-min sessions, adjust anytime"

3. REASSURANCE THROUGH LANGUAGE
   "I believe cost shouldn't be a barrier" (values alignment)
   "We'll discuss what works for you" (partnership, not prescription)
   "Many of my clients use superbills" (social proof)

4. PLACEMENT AT PEAK DOUBT
   Between form + details (high-friction zone)
   Catches hesitation at decision point
   Removes final objections before commitment

5. INTERACTIVE UX = LOWER COGNITIVE LOAD
   Hidden answers (expandable) don't overwhelm
   Click-to-reveal gives user control
   Quick answers address objection immediately
