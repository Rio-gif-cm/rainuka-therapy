Palette Comparison

✨ New Palette (Burgundy/Deep Gray/Sea Blue)

Primary
Burgundy - Warm, welcoming, sophisticated
Secondary
Deep Gray - Professional, grounded
Accent
Sea Blue - Calming, trustworthy

Impression: Warm burgundy feels inviting and intimate (works for all genders). Deep gray adds serious professional weight. Sea blue provides a calming, trustworthy accent that doesn't feel jarring.

🌿 Original Palette (Sage/Warm Gray)

Primary
Sage - Natural, calming
Secondary
Warm Gray - Neutral, warm
Accent
Clay - Warm, invitation

Impression: Sage green is calming but may read as "wellness/eco" to some. Warm gray is very neutral. Clay adds warmth but is distinctly warm-toned (may feel more "feminine").

Professional Assessment

Does it look professional?

✅ YES – Burgundy (#6d3a47) is a sophisticated, premium color used in luxury and healthcare brands. Deep gray adds executive weight. Sea blue provides a professional, calming contrast.

Does it work for BOTH men AND women?

✅ STRONGER YES – The burgundy + deep gray + sea blue combination is more gender-neutral than sage + warm gray:

• Burgundy is deep and sophisticated without leaning heavily feminine (unlike lighter pinks or pastels)
• Deep Gray adds masculine/professional weight
• Sea Blue is universally calming without gender associations

The sage palette, while beautiful, can read slightly "feminine" or "wellness-oriented" to some viewers, especially for male clients seeking serious therapy. This palette feels more inclusive and professional across genders.

Concerns?

Minor: The burgundy is darker and bolder than sage—ensure it maintains enough contrast for accessibility in text scenarios. Test on smaller text to ensure legibility. The sea blue button contrasts well with the burgundy background.

🎯 RECOMMENDATION

KEEP THE NEW COLORS

The burgundy/deep gray/sea blue palette is more professional, more gender-inclusive, and creates better visual hierarchy than sage/warm gray. It maintains sophistication while feeling warmer and more inviting than a pure gray system. The sea blue accent provides necessary visual relief and signals trust/calm without competing with the brand color.
