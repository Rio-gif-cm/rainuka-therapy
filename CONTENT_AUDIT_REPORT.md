The Rainuka Therapy website contains **32 pages** with strong foundational content across core specializations (perinatal, ADHD, career) and accessibility/inclusion. ## Critical Content Gaps Identified

### 1. **Blog/Editorial Strategy Missing** 📝
- **Issue**: Guides exist but are scattered. No clear content strategy/calendar.
- **Impact**: Lost SEO opportunity. No "educational" content attracting search traffic.
- **Solution Needed**: Central blog hub, content categories, publication schedule.

### 2. **Niche-Specific Deep-Dives Underdeveloped** 🎯
- **Issue**: ADHD, Perinatal, Career pages cover basics but lack **advanced resource pages**.
- **Examples Missing**:
  - "ADHD Medication and Therapy: How They Work Together" (educational, high-search volume)
  - "Postpartum Anxiety vs. Postpartum Depression: How to Tell" (distinct conditions, FAQs conflate)
  - "Job Search Anxiety: Strategies for Neurodivergent Professionals" (underserved niche)

### 3. **Guides Lack Depth & Organization** 📚
- **Issue**: Guides exist but no internal linking, category structure, or progression.
- **Current Guides Count**: ~4-5 (unclear exact count from code review)
- **Needed**: 15+ comprehensive guides across specializations with SEO strategy.

### 4. **FAQ Coverage Gaps** ❓
- **Missing FAQs**:
  - Trauma & ADHD comorbidity
  - Medication questions (antidepressants, ADHD meds, pregnancy safety)
  - Partner/couple communication
  - "How do I know if I need therapy?" (self-assessment)
  - Therapy outcomes & timeframes
  - Termination & long-term clients

### 5. **Resources Page Weak** 🔗
- **Issue**: Single perinatal resource page. No central hub for tools, worksheets, apps, external resources.
- **Opportunity**: Create multi-category resource library with:
  - ADHD assessment tools
  - Perinatal support hotlines
  - Couples communication worksheets
  - Career planning templates

### 6. **Educational/Evergreen Content Thin** 📖
- **Issue**: Most pages are service/sales pages. Very little educational content that *leads* to booking.
- **Missing**:
  - "What to Expect in Your First Therapy Session" (detailed prep guide)
  - "Therapy Myths Debunked" (objection handling via education)
  - "How Trauma-Informed Therapy Works" (explain modality)
  - Glossary of therapy terms

### 7. **Specialty Crossover Pages Missing** 🔀
- **Issue**: No pages for dual specializations (e.g., "Perinatal ADHD," "Career After Loss," "Couples & Neurodivergence")
- **Impact**: Clients with overlapping needs can't find targeted pages.
- **SEO Opportunity**: Long-tail keywords underserved.

### 8. **Success Stories & Outcomes Underdeveloped** 📈
- **Issue**: Testimonials exist but no "transformation journey" narratives.
- **Missing**: Before/after stories that show progression (not just quotes).

### 9. **Accessibility/Inclusion Content Limited** ♿
- **Issue**: Have 4 dedicated pages (disability, BIPOC, LGBTQ+, neurodivergent) but each could have:
  - Dedicated FAQ
  - Specific guides
  - Resource links
  - Community recommendations

### 10. **Technical/Booking Friction** 🚧
- **Issues in Booking Flow**:
  - No "why free consultation is valuable" deep copy
  - No "I'm nervous" reassurance
  - Contact page lacks email response time SLA clarity
  - No "best way to reach out" guidance

## Conclusion

The Rainuka Therapy website has strong **core content** (About, Pricing, Specialties) but lacks **supporting educational content** that improves SEO, reduces booking friction, and establishes thought leadership.

**Priority:** Fill the guides/blog infrastructure gap and create crossover specialty pages. These additions will:
- ✅ Improve organic search visibility (estimated +30-50% organic traffic)
- ✅ Reduce booking friction (more informed, confident leads)
- ✅ Establish authority in underserved niches
- ✅ Create internal linking structure (improves SEO)

**Estimated Effort:** 40-60 hours over 12 weeks (2-4 hours/week)  
**ROI:** High—educational content compounds in SEO value over time and improves conversion quality.
