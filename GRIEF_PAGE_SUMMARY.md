## What Was Built

### Page: `/grief` (app/grief/page.tsx)

**Research-Backed Content on:**

1. **Grief Normalization** — "Grief is love with nowhere to go"
   - Validates grief as normal, healthy response (not depression/anxiety)
   - Addresses shame and societal pressure to "move on"

2. **Six Types of Grief** (with descriptions)
   - Bereavement (death of loved ones)
   - Pregnancy Loss & Miscarriage (invisible grief)
   - Pet Loss (disenfranchised but deeply real)
   - Disenfranchised Grief (losses society doesn't acknowledge: estrangement, friendship loss, suicide, identity loss)
   - Anticipatory Grief (grief before the loss happens)
   - Ambiguous Loss (grief without closure: dementia, missing persons, chronic illness)

3. **Non-Linear Grief Timeline** (4 phases, not linear stages)
   - Weeks 1-4: Shock & Acute Grief (numbness, disbelief, autopilot)
   - Months 2-6: "Waves" & Reality Setting In (triggers, anger, guilt, waves of intensity)
   - Months 6-12+: Integration & Continued Adaptation (pain becomes chronic but bearable)
   - Year 2+: Permanent Change, Not "Moving On" (grief becomes integrated into identity)
   - ⚠️ Complicated Grief warning (when to seek help)

4. **Therapeutic Approaches** (4 evidence-based methods)
   - **Narrative Therapy**: Restoring your story, separating grief from identity, finding "unique outcomes"
   - **Meaning-Making**: Finding "Why does this matter?" (not just "Why did this happen?"), connecting to legacy
   - **Dual Process Model**: Oscillating between loss-oriented and restoration-oriented coping
   - **Continuing Bonds**: They're always part of your life, finding new relationship with them

5. **Support Beyond Therapy**
   - Grief Support Groups (Grief Share, The Dinner Party, GriefShare Online, HUGS)
   - Specialized Resources (pet loss, miscarriage, infertility, suicide, MMIWG)
   - Books & Meaning-Making (Joan Didion, Paul Kalanithi, Megan Devine, Frank Ostaseski)
   - Online Communities (r/grief, GriefNet, Soaring Spirits)
   - Crisis Resources (988, Crisis Text Line, international support)

6. **Grief FAQ** (12 questions addressing common concerns)
   - "How long will this take? Will I ever feel normal again?"
   - "I'm angry all the time. Is that normal?"
   - "I'm laughing and having fun—does that mean I didn't love them?"
   - "I feel like I'm supposed to be better by now"
   - "What if I'm grieving someone with a complicated relationship?"
   - "I don't feel anything. Am I broken?"
   - "Should I remove their photos/belongings?"
   - "People keep saying dismissive platitudes"
   - "I'm having thoughts about joining them" (crisis support)
   - "My grief is making my relationships harder"
   - "Is it disrespectful to move on/start dating again?"
   - "My pet died and people say 'it was just a pet'"

## Content Strategy

### Tone & Language
- **Trauma-informed:** Validates without minimizing
- **Specific, not generic:** "Postpartum anxiety" not "stress"
- **Metaphor-rich:** "Grief is love with nowhere to go"
- **Myth-busting:** Addresses common misconceptions (5 stages are linear, you "move on", etc.)
- **Compassionate but honest:** Doesn't sugarcoat loss or timeline
- **Inclusive:** Honors disenfranchised grief, complicated relationships, diverse loss types

### High-Intent Messaging
- Target audience: People experiencing ANY loss (bereavement, miscarriage, pet loss, estrangement, identity loss)
- Positioning: "Your grief is normal. Your loss matters. You deserve support."
- Conversion: Free 30-min call to process, no pressure

## Underserved Niche Addressed

**Why this page matters:**
- Grief & loss is common but underserved by therapy websites
- Most therapy sites either avoid grief or treat it as one checkbox
- This page honors:
  - ✅ Disenfranchised grief (pet loss, miscarriage, estrangement) — often invisible socially
  - ✅ Non-linear, personalized grief — not Kübler-Ross 5 stages
  - ✅ Different loss types with specificity
  - ✅ Modern therapeutic approaches (narrative therapy, meaning-making)
  - ✅ Continuing bonds (they don't disappear, relationship transforms)
