Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about therapy, confidentiality, pricing, and my specialties in perinatal mental health, ADHD, and career counseling.

About Therapy

Is this first-time therapy?▼

It doesn't matter—whether you've been before or this is your first time, I meet you where you are with no judgment.

Will you judge me?▼

No. My job is to understand, not judge. Everything you share stays confidential and judgment-free.

How is therapy different from talking to friends?▼

Friends care but aren't trained to help you process emotions or build new patterns. Therapy offers specialized support with confidentiality and safety.

Will you tell me what to do?▼

No. I help you think clearly so you can decide. My role is support and guidance, not direction.

How long does therapy typically take?▼

It varies. Some benefit from 6–8 sessions; others work with me for months or years. We'll figure out what's right for you.

Practical Information

How much does therapy cost?▼

Standard rate is $150/session for 50-minute therapy. I offer sliding scale ($80–$120) for limited slots based on financial need. For detailed pricing, insurance info, and payment options, see our pricing page.

Do you take insurance?▼

I'm in-network with several insurance providers. I also offer superbills for out-of-network reimbursement, which can reduce your out-of-pocket cost significantly. Ask during your free call.

What if I can't afford $150/session?▼

We have several options: sliding scale (if slots are available), out-of-network insurance reimbursement, payment plans, or resources elsewhere. Financial barriers shouldn't prevent you from getting help.

Can I get reimbursed by insurance if you're out-of-network?▼

Yes, if you have out-of-network (OON) mental health benefits. I provide a superbill (detailed receipt), you submit it to insurance, and they reimburse you directly. Many clients go from $150 out-of-pocket to just $50–$75 effective cost.

How often do people typically meet?▼

Most start with weekly 50-minute sessions and adjust to bi-weekly after establishing momentum.

Is telehealth as effective as in-person?▼

Research confirms yes. You stay comfortable, avoid commute, and the connection is real.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Is therapy confidential?▼

Yes—everything is protected by HIPAA and therapist-client privilege, except mandatory reporting situations (risk of harm).

What happens if you think I'm a danger to myself or others?▼

I'm required by law to take action (emergency services, family contact, or authorities). We'll discuss this during your first session.

Do you use my information for anything else?▼

No. Your information stays secure and is used only for your care—never for marketing or research.

What's your data security like?▼

Sessions use HIPAA-compliant, encrypted video. Notes are stored securely with full data protection.

About My Specialties

Do I need to have a diagnosis to work with you?▼

No. You don't need an ADHD or perinatal diagnosis to explore those concerns. We work with what's happening for you.

Is postpartum depression/anxiety real?▼

Absolutely. Perinatal mental health challenges are real, common, treatable—and you're not weak or broken.

Can I do therapy while pregnant?▼

Yes. Therapy during pregnancy helps with anxiety, processing fears, and preparing for birth and postpartum.

Will therapy help with ADHD symptoms?▼

Therapy complements medication by helping you understand ADHD, build systems that work, and process shame.

Should I quit my job before starting therapy?▼

Not necessarily. Let's talk through what's really happening first. Career therapy helps you decide, not pushes an answer.
