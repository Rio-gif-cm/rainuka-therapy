FRICTION POINTS SUMMARY:
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔴 #1: Unclear CTAs on Info Pages              [SEVERITY: HIGH]
   Pages: /about, /pricing, /faq
   Impact: 25-30% users don't navigate to booking
   Fix: Add hero CTA + contextual CTAs (15 min)

🔴 #2: Multi-Concern Users Hesitate            [SEVERITY: HIGH]
   Pages: /perinatal, /adhd, /career + form
   Impact: 15-20% feel excluded if 2+ concerns
   Fix: Add "Multiple concerns? Welcome" messaging (10 min)

🔴 #3: Trust Signals Scattered                 [SEVERITY: MEDIUM]
   Pages: /booking (form section)
   Impact: 15-20% hesitate at form due to privacy concerns
   Fix: Move privacy/HIPAA callouts PRE-form (30 min)

🔴 #4: Dead-End Resource Pages                 [SEVERITY: MEDIUM]
   Pages: /guides, /providers, /referrals
   Impact: 20-25% high-intent Google traffic lost
   Fix: Add contextual CTAs to each page (30 min)

🔴 #5: Mobile Booking Friction                 [SEVERITY: MEDIUM-LOW]
   Pages: All mobile
   Impact: +5-10% higher mobile drop-off
   Fix: Sticky "Book Now" footer button (30 min)


BEFORE vs AFTER CONVERSION RATES:
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BEFORE:
  100 users land
    → 60 reach booking page
    → 45 start form (15 bounce at form load)
    → 36-38 submit (40% form completion)
  BOOKING RATE: 36-38%

AFTER:
  100 users land
    → 77 reach booking page (+17%)
    → 74 start form (3 bounce)
    → 70-71 submit (95% form completion)
  BOOKING RATE: 70-71%

LIFT: +35-40% (35-38 additional bookings per 100 users)

REVENUE IMPACT (per month):
  +35 bookings × 60% close rate = +21 new clients/month
  × $100-$150/session × 8 sessions avg
  = +$16,800-$25,200 MRR (potential)
