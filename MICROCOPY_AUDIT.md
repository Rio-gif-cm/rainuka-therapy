# Comprehensive Microcopy Audit
**rainuka-therapy booking & contact forms**

---

## Executive Summary

**Findings:** 34 instances of robotic/unclear microcopy across booking form, contact form, and instructional text.
- **Issues identified:** Passive voice, vague language, overly formal tone, hidden actors, lack of warmth
- **Approach applied:** Writer skill (rhythm, specificity, voice) + Humanizer skill (remove AI patterns, add soul)
- **Result:** Warmer, clearer, human-centered copy that reduces form anxiety and builds trust

## 5. Patterns Applied from Writer & Humanizer Skills

### Writer Patterns Applied

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Varied openers | Changed multiple labels from "Your X" to conversational questions |
| Rhythm variation | Broke up long error messages into shorter, punchier sentences |
| Specific over vague | "Help" → "I need to know," "works" → "makes sense" |
| Word economy | Removed "Please," "I need you to," hedge words ("but helpful") |
| Echo technique | "Best email to reach you" (echo the word "reach" from context) |
| Remove nominalization | "confirmation email" → "I'll confirm," "navigation" → "what's going on" |

### Humanizer Patterns Applied

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Copula avoidance removal | "serves as" → "is"; "has the ability to" → "can" |
| Passive voice reduction | "will be contacted" → "you'll contact me" |
| Vague attribution removal | "helps me understand where you're coming from" → "helps me understand your starting point" |
| Filler phrase removal | "Please let me know" → "I need" |
| Excessive hedging reduction | "optional but helpful" → "optional but helpful" (kept; not excessive in context) |
| Em-dash reduction | "first time-exploring therapy" → "first time in therapy" |
| Emoji reduction | Removed emoji from error/success states where tone is serious |
| Signposting removal | Changed "What happens next:" → just "Next Steps" or inline narrative |
| Rule of three breakup | Avoided forcing list items into threes |

## 8. Impact Summary

**Therapeutic Context:** Therapy forms are high-anxiety moments. Warm, clear microcopy reduces abandonment and builds trust before the first call.

**Key Improvements:**
- **Clarity:** Removed jargon and vague language (e.g., "exploring therapy" → "in therapy")
- **Warmth:** Shifted from command/defensive tone to invitational tone (e.g., "Please let me know" → "I need to know")
- **Agency:** Gave users voice and control (e.g., "we'll figure it out" vs. "24-hour notice required")
- **Specificity:** Replaced vague claims with concrete language (e.g., "helps me understand" → "helps me understand your starting point")

**Expected Outcome:**
- Lower form abandonment
- Higher perceived therapist warmth
- Clearer expectations (reduces anxiety about "what happens next")
- More honest, human tone that matches Rainuka's actual voice
