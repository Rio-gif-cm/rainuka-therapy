## THE PROBLEM (From Auditor Feedback)

Three critical SEO gaps are suppressing conversions by 25-40%:

1. **Conversion Intent Mismatch** (SEO Auditor #9)
   - Users searching for specific problems (cost, availability, ADHD diagnosis) land on vague homepage
   - High-intent keywords → generic landing pages → users bounce to competitors
   - **Impact:** 20-35% conversion loss

2. **Content Depth Deficit** (SEO Auditor #8)
   - Competitors rank for 50-500+ ADHD/perinatal keywords; Rainuka ranks for ~0
   - BetterHelp has 50+ blog posts on ADHD; Rainuka has 0
   - Domain Authority 28 vs. competitors 65-93 (massive gap)
   - **Impact:** Invisible in AI search + organic rankings

3. **Landing Page & UX Friction** (SEO Auditor #7)
   - Objections (cost, what to expect, am I ready?) cleared AFTER CTA, not before
   - Hero CTAs missing on specialty pages (below fold)
   - FAQ schema missing (15-25% CTR loss on rich snippets)
   - **Impact:** 25-30% abandonment rate

## THE SOLUTION: TIERED CONTENT ROADMAP

### Tier 1: Critical Pages (4 weeks) — **ROI: 10/10**
Launch 5 conversion-critical pages addressing specific auditor gaps:

1. **"First Therapy Session: What to Expect"** (1,200 words)
   - Clears biggest objection blocking bookings (therapy anxiety)
   - Embed on booking page ABOVE form
   - Est. 25-30% booking lift from objection clearing

2. **"Therapy Myths Debunked"** (1,200 words + blog series)
   - Addresses skepticism keywords (unranked currently)
   - Builds trust for hesitant prospects
   - Est. 15-20% skepticism-to-booking conversion

3. **"Cost, Insurance, Timeline"** (1,200 words + booking page embed)
   - Removes friction by answering before CTA
   - Addresses "price research" keyword cluster
   - Est. 25-35% objection clearing

4. **"ADHD + Postpartum: Why Late Diagnosis Matters"** (2,000 words, specialty page)
   - Addresses blind spot: 60%+ postpartum ADHD undiagnosed
   - Zero content currently; competitors dominate
   - New keyword opportunity: 800-1,200 searches/month
   - Est. 30-40% segment lift

5. **"ADHD High-Performers"** (1,500 words, specialty page)
   - Speaks to Rainuka's core audience
   - High-income, high-intent segment
   - Est. 35-45% high-performer conversion

**Tier 1 Traffic Estimate:** +500-800 sessions/month (first 4 weeks)

### Tier 2: Authority Building (Weeks 3-5) — **ROI: 8/10**
3 specialty crossover pages + 1 educational blog:

1. **"ADHD + Couples Relationships"** (2,000 words)
   - Unaddressed keyword: "ADHD couples therapy"
   - Crosslinks ADHD + Couples specialties
   - Est. 20-25% couples segment lift

2. **"Career After Loss"** (2,000 words)
   - Career + trauma specialty crossover
   - Est. 15-20% trauma-to-career conversion

3. **"ADHD Medication + Therapy: How They Work Together"** (1,800 words, blog)
   - High-intent search: "ADHD meds + therapy"
   - Educational + objection clearing
   - Est. 20-25% medication-hesitant segment

4. **"Perinatal ADHD: Identity Integration"** (2,500 words, deep-dive)
   - Deep-dive on ADHD identity in motherhood (distinct from Tier 1.4)
   - Est. 25-30% deep-dive reader-to-booking

**Tier 2 Traffic Estimate:** +250-400 sessions/month (cumulative)

### Tier 3: Long-Tail SEO (Weeks 6-12) — **ROI: 6-7/10**
6 seasonal blog posts + technical optimization:

1. Seasonal blog series (6 posts, 2h each)
   - Summer transitions, holiday burnout, return to work, career reassessment, ADHD burnout, New Year resolutions
   - Build topical authority + long-tail keyword coverage
   - Est. +150-250 sessions/month from blog authority

2. FAQ schema markup on all FAQ pages
   - Fix critical gap: No FAQPage schema (15-25% CTR loss)
   - Rich snippets on FAQ queries
   - Est. +100-200 CTR lift from schema

**Tier 3 Traffic Estimate:** +200-300 sessions/month (cumulative)
