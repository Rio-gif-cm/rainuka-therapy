## WHAT WE'RE PROPOSING: NICHE POSITIONING STRATEGY

### Current Problem (Generic Positioning)
"Trauma-informed therapist for ADHD, perinatal, and career"
- **Issue:** 500+ Bay Area therapists claim this exact positioning
- **Result:** Zero differentiation, zero SEO advantage

### Winning Approach (Niche Authority)
**"ADHD diagnosis specialist for high-achieving women in perinatal transition"**

#### Why This Wins:
1. **Rare niche:** ~5-10 therapists in Bay Area vs. 500 generalists
2. **High commercial intent:** Women actively searching for this exact help
3. **Defensible positioning:** Competitors don't focus on ADHD + perinatal + career combo
4. **AI search advantage:** Specific niches rank better than broad services
5. **Authentic to Rainuka:** Aligns with your actual expertise (ADHD + motherhood + career)

#### Three Content Pillars:
1. **ADHD + Perinatal Intersection** — "Why ADHD diagnosis comes during motherhood"
2. **ADHD + Career** — "Career therapy for ADHD + professional transitions"
3. **Postpartum ADHD** — "Late ADHD diagnosis and identity reconstruction"
