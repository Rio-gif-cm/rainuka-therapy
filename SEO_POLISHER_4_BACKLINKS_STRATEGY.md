## 💡 ANCHOR TEXT STRATEGY (Detailed)

### Distribution Target (After 50-80 Links)
- **40% Branded:** "Rainuka Therapy," "Rainuka," "Rainuka Perinatal" (builds brand authority)
- **35% Descriptive:** "ADHD therapy," "Perinatal therapy," "Career counseling SF" (builds keyword signals)
- **15% URL:** Direct links (builds domain authority)
- **10% Generic:** "Therapy," "Learn more," "Read more" (natural distribution)

### Anchor Text Examples by Context

| Specialty | Branded | Descriptive | Context |
|-----------|---------|-------------|---------|
| **ADHD** | "Rainuka ADHD Therapy" | "Adult ADHD therapy," "Late ADHD diagnosis specialist" | Guest posts, directories |
| **Perinatal** | "Rainuka Perinatal" | "Postpartum anxiety therapy," "Perinatal depression support" | Health blogs, PSI |
| **Career** | "Rainuka Career Therapy" | "Career burnout counseling," "Job transition therapy" | Career blogs, business sites |
| **Local** | "Rainuka SF" | "ADHD therapist San Francisco," "Perinatal therapy Bay Area" | Local directories |

### What to AVOID
❌ Exact-match keyword spam ("online ADHD therapy diagnose quickly")  
❌ Clickbait anchor text ("Click here," "This link")  
❌ Irrelevant keywords (therapy site linking with "cheap flights" anchor)  
❌ Over-optimization (>20% of links with single keyword phrase)
