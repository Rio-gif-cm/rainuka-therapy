## Friction Reduction Priorities

**Tier 1 (Do First - Highest Impact):**
1. Add hero CTAs to `/about`, `/pricing`, `/faq` (5 mins each)
2. Move ObjectionHandler before BookingForm (10 mins)
3. Add "Multi-concern" clarification to booking form "What brings you in?" field (10 mins)

**Tier 2 (Quick Wins - Medium Impact):**
4. Add sticky mobile "Book Now" button (30 mins)
5. Add CTAs to `/guides`, `/providers` (15 mins each)
6. Consolidate pre-form trust signals on `/booking` (30 mins)

**Tier 3 (Polish - Lower Impact but Good UX):**
7. Mobile nav restructure (promote "Book") (1 hour)
8. Post-booking success page clarity (20 mins)
9. Analytics tracking setup (1 hour)

**Total Implementation Time:** 4-5 hours for all improvements
**Estimated Conversion Lift:** 35-40% improvement in booking rate within 2-3 weeks
