KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
  • Sage (burgundy) over-used at 72% of all accents
  • Sea-blue under-utilized (2 usages vs. intended 40-60)
  • Deep-gray undefined purpose (3 usages vs. intended 20-30)
  • 139 readability concerns (accents in body text, non-ideal backgrounds)
  • No clear visual hierarchy (can't distinguish primary/secondary CTAs)

ROOT CAUSE:
  • Design system is well-defined but poorly applied
  • Sage creep into forms, labels, info boxes, body text
  • Sea-blue never implemented as secondary CTA
  • No documented standards for accent usage

IMPACT:
  • Accents are FORCED, not OPTIMAL
  • CTAs lose focal power due to widespread sage usage
  • Visitor can't distinguish primary from secondary actions
  • Professional finish diluted by over-branding
