# Blog Foundation Wave 1: Research & Strategy

## Blog Effectiveness for Therapy Practices

### Key Metrics
- **Lead Generation:** Content marketing produces 3x more leads than traditional marketing, 62% less cost
- **Trust Building:** 63% of marketers confirm valuable content builds long-lasting client loyalty
- **Organic Traffic:** 53% of therapy practice traffic comes from organic search; 80%+ of clients start therapy search online
- **Search Visibility:** Top search result captures ~32% of all clicks — ranking matters significantly

### Recommended Blog Post Length
- **Standard blog posts (lead generation):** 1,200–1,600 words
- **Cornerstone content (in-depth guides):** ~2,000 words
- **Structure matters:** H1/H2/H3 headings, short paragraphs, bullet points, scannable sections

## Content Calendar Strategy

### Frequency & Planning
- **Consistency over quantity:** 1–2 posts per month, on a predictable schedule
- **Content calendar:** Map topics 2–3 months in advance
- **Avoid burnout:** Pre-plan reduces weekly "what to write?" friction
- **Keyword spacing:** Structure calendar to cover diverse searches & specialties over time

## Content Strategy Framework

### Tone & Voice
- **Warm, accessible, demystifying** (opposite of clinical jargon)
- **Normalize the struggle** (perinatal anxiety is real; late ADHD diagnosis is painful; burnout is valid)
- **Affirm identity** (you're not broken; you're discovering yourself)

### Structure for First Post (Sample)
1. **Hook:** "You have a booking. Now what?" (relatable anxiety)
2. **Validation:** "First-session nerves are universal—here's what's really happening"
3. **What to Bring** (practical)
4. **What to Expect** (structure + timelines)
5. **FAQ** (privacy, cost, What if I cry?, etc.)
6. **CTA** (soft: "Ready to book? Questions? Email us.")
