# ADHD-Friendly Web Design Principles for Therapy Pages

## Key Insights

### Cognitive Load Reduction (Critical for ADHD)
- Cognitive load is the mental effort required to process information on screen
- ADHD users struggle with high cognitive load → task abandonment & anxiety
- 15-20% of global population is neurodivergent; therapy pages need to account for this
- Five practical principles:
  1. Clear, consistent navigation patterns
  2. Minimize distractions (auto-play, flashing, animation)
  3. Break tasks into simple, manageable steps
  4. Customization options (text size, contrast)
  5. Key actions/info must be easy to find

### Visual Hierarchy & Scannability
- Users spend ~5.59 seconds on written content (scanning, not reading)
- Effective hierarchy works WITH scanning behavior
- Elements: Size, color, whitespace, proximity, contrast
- Google homepage example: single search bar + vast whitespace = zero cognitive load

### Common Barriers That Increase Cognitive Load
- Distracting animations & auto-playing videos
- Dense layouts
- Inconsistent navigation
- Unclear CTAs
- Jargon-heavy content
- Multiple competing focal points

## Late-Diagnosis Shame & Psychology

### Emotional Impact
- Late ADHD diagnosis brings **grief, relief, confusion** (not just relief)
- Grief for: lost time, self-blame, years spent unmasking
- Shame-based language ("fix yourself," "overcome," "optimize") triggers old wounds
- After years of being told "try harder," the therapy page's words carry real emotional weight

### Client Decision-Making Pattern
- ADHD clients ask: "Does this feel respectful or like pressure to perform?"
- They notice shame-coded language INSTANTLY
- Examples that sting: "fix procrastination," "stop being distractible," "finally be consistent"
- They respond to: alignment, strengths recognition, lived understanding

---

## Neurodivergent-Affirming Marketing Copy

### Core Shift: Drop "Fixing" Language, Promise Fit & Alignment
Instead of: "Fix your ADHD," "overcome your diagnosis," "optimize your brain"
Use: "We work with how your brain already works," "build structures that make starting easier"

### Language Patterns That Convert
- ❌ "Fix procrastination once and for all"
- ✅ "Build low-friction routines that support your attention"

- ❌ "Stop being so distractible"
- ✅ "Experiment with structures that make starting easier"

- ❌ "Finally become consistent"
- ✅ "Shape systems around your real energy"

### Lead with Strengths (Not Pain)
Many ND clients respond to recognition of what already works:
- Pattern-spotting
- Creativity
- Honesty & directness
- Deep focus (hyperfocus)
- Sensitivity & empathy
- Humor
- Persistence
- Unusual problem-solving

## Design Patterns: Reduced Visual Complexity

### Layout Simplification
- Minimize density; increase whitespace (40-50% is ideal for anxiety reduction)
- Use chunked information (bullet points, short paragraphs)
- Consistent visual structure across sections
- Clear content boundaries

### CTA Hierarchy Clarity
- One primary CTA per section (not multiple competing actions)
- High contrast buttons
- Action verbs that feel supportive, not demanding
- Placement: easy to find without overwhelming

### Sections to Remove/Reduce
- Unnecessary animations (can trigger distraction/overwhelm)
- Auto-playing videos
- Flashing or rapid-change content
- Dense credential lists (builds distrust, not trust)
- Multiple competing value props on same section

---

## Decision-Making Patterns for ADHD Therapy Clients

- **High friction = abandonment**: Long forms, unclear next steps, confusing navigation
- **Executive function barriers**: Require clear sequencing, specific next steps, low cognitive load
- **Trust signals matter**: Warmth, specificity, naming their experience, social proof
- **Action bias**: They're ready to book; remove friction, not add steps
- **Fear of judgment**: Shame-aware copy + normalization of late diagnosis = safety signal

---

## Actionable Takeaways for Rainuka's ADHD Page

1. **Add social proof section**: Client testimonials or statistics showing ADHD late-diagnosis is common
2. **Simplify CTA hierarchy**: Move from "Book a Free Consultation" repeating 3x to ONE clear primary CTA + optional secondary
3. **Reduce visual noise**: Minimize animations, increase whitespace
4. **Shame-reduction language**: Audit copy for "fix," "overcome," "optimize" → replace with "understand," "align," "build"
5. **Clear next-step clarity**: Make it obvious what happens after clicking (not vague)
6. **Confidence building**: Add line about what % of ADHD diagnoses are late, how common late diagnosis is for women
