# Multi-Persona Discovery in Therapy Marketing

## Key Research Findings

### The Problem: Client Uncertainty in Niche-Heavy Sites
Research shows clients don't always know which persona they are:
- **Overlapping needs**: Postpartum clients often have undiagnosed ADHD + career concerns
- **ADHD diagnosis at 35+**: Creates both relief AND grief — identity confusion
- **Career transitions**: Often involve trauma, burnout, anxiety — multiple frameworks apply
- **Decision friction**: Clients spend <30 seconds deciding if site is "for them"

**Source**: GTM Playbook for Counseling Practices (Pulse RevOps, 2027) — niche positioning requires clarity, but therapists serve multiple intersecting populations.

### The Opportunity: Multi-Persona Discovery UX
Therapy websites typically present 1-3 niches as separate cards. **Better pattern:**
1. Hero section asks: "Which applies to you?" (low-friction selector)
2. Reveals multiple personas at once
3. Clients can toggle through overlapping needs
4. Reduces exit friction ("This site isn't for me" → "Let me check the others")

### Design Pattern Insight
From niche-positioning research (Enlight Labs, 2024):
- Fewer, more targeted visitors → higher conversion (10% vs 2%)
- BUT: On a site with 3 distinct niches, **ambiguous positioning kills all three**
- Solution: Let clients self-select FIRST, then present comparison

### Tactical Implementation
**"Which applies to you?" selector in hero:**
- Three buttons/toggles (Perinatal | ADHD | Career)
- No form submission required
- Reveals expanded content for selected persona
- "Explore other specialties" link if user changes mind
- Mobile-optimized (stack vertically)

### Overlapping Needs Messaging
Key insight: Clients with comorbid issues need reassurance that Rainuka can handle complexity:
- "You might see yourself in more than one—that's exactly why I specialize in what feels stuck"
- "Postpartum anxiety + undiagnosed ADHD? Career change while managing grief? You're not alone."
- Reference during intake: "Many clients come in with overlapping needs—we work with what's actually happening."

## Actionable Takeaways

✅ **Add selector in hero**: "What brings you here?" (multi-select visual)
✅ **Expand niche cards dynamically**: Show full comparison when selected
✅ **Add "Explore other specialties" CTA**: Reduce exit friction
✅ **Reassurance copy for comorbidity**: "You might be multiple of these—that's OK"
✅ **Test on mobile first**: 65%+ of therapy-seekers browse on phone
