# Rainuka Therapy Brand Voice Style Guide
**For Content Writers | 1-page Reference**

---

## Core Identity: "The Therapist Who Understands"

**Your voice is:**
- ✅ Warm but direct
- ✅ Validating, not platitudinous
- ✅ Non-prescriptive (clarify, don't tell)
- ✅ Complexity-honoring (both/and, not either/or)
- ✅ Shame-reframing (different, not broken)

**Your voice is NOT:**
- ❌ Clinical jargon (except when teaching)
- ❌ Prescriptive ("here's what you should do")
- ❌ Bubbly or performative
- ❌ Dismissive of contradiction
- ❌ Focused on fixing/coping/managing (old therapy speak)

---

## Tone by Context

| **Context** | **Warmth** | **Directness** | **Key Move** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Perinatal/ADHD** | Very High | High | Validate grief + name mechanism |
| **Career** | Moderate-High | Very High | Clarify without prescribing |
| **Booking** | Moderate | High | Remove friction, build trust |
| **Homepage** | High | Moderate | Invite, not sell |

## Language Patterns

### 1. **The Short-Affirm + Explain Pattern**
Affirm with 1-3 short words/phrases. Then expand with mechanism or validation.

**Examples:**
- "Your grief is real. Your anxiety is real. Both coexist with joy."
- "Different, not broken. We'll build on your strengths."
- "That's data. Not laziness—data."

**When to use:** Any time you're reframing shame or validating contradiction.

### 2. **Name the Mechanism, Not the Fix**
Help readers *understand* why, not *how to fix* it.

**Good:**
- "Perinatal hormones rewire your brain. Your emotions aren't random—they're a response."
- "Shame is inherited, not intrinsic."
- "Time blindness is executive function difference, not a character flaw."

**Avoid:**
- "Tips to manage anxiety"
- "5 ways to beat ADHD"
- "Overcome your perfectionism"

### 3. **Hold Contradiction**
Both/and, not either/or. Name opposing feelings as *both* real.

**Good:**
- "Love and space coexist."
- "Grief and relief coexist."
- "Love your baby and resent the loss of freedom. All real, all true."

**Avoid:**
- "Despite the grief, you'll feel joy."
- "You can move past this."
- "Focus on the positive."

### 4. **Reframe Language**
Replace deficit language with neutral or affirming framing.

| **Avoid** | **Use Instead** |
|---|---|
| Fix, overcome, manage, cope | Understand, clarify, process, integrate |
| Disorder, dysfunction | Difference, mechanism, pattern |
| Broken, failing, weak | Unmet need, unrecognized struggle, system hit its limit |
| Healing | Integration, reconstruction, reclaiming |

### 5. **The Permission Move**
Give explicit permission for contradictions, emotions, boundaries.

**Good:**
- "Anger, despair, hope—all belong here."
- "You don't need a diagnosis to start."
- "Judgment-free. Your pace."

**Avoid:**
- "It's okay to feel..." (sounds patronizing)
- "Don't worry about..." (dismissive)

## Vocabulary: Rainuka's Toolkit

### **Frequently Used (Deploy Liberally)**
- Real, validate, understand, navigate, clarify
- Pattern, mechanism, data, response, adaptation
- Ground, space, pace, permission
- Both, and, coexist, integrate

### **Rarely Used (Use Sparingly)**
- Resilience (overused in therapy speak)
- Mindfulness, healing, growth (too abstract)
- Trauma, trigger (use only when clinically precise)

### **Never Use**
- "Toxic," "red flag" (pop-psychology)
- "Journey," "season" (vague)
- Inspirational clichés ("you've got this," "be brave")

## Sentence Structure Signature

**Sentence Length:** Mix short + long.
- Short sentences (1-3 words) for affirmation/reframe
- Longer sentences (15-25 words) for explanation
- Never more than 2 sentences without a paragraph break

**Rhythm:** Observation → Validation → Mechanism → Permission → Clarity

**Punctuation:** 
- Use periods over exclamation marks (let weight land)
- Dashes (—) for expansion, parentheses (rarely)
- Colons for lists that follow a principle

## Specific Moves by Page Type

### **Specialty Pages (Perinatal, ADHD, Career)**
1. **Hero:** Emotive headline + lead sentence that names the unsaid
2. **"Who this is for":** Bullet-list validation, not diagnosis
3. **"Why this works":** Mechanism (brain science, identity shifts, burnout data)
4. **"What we do":** Outcome-focused, never prescriptive
5. **Testimonials:** Lead with feeling/change, not "success"

### **Homepage**
1. Lead with "what you actually need," not services
2. Use metaphor (grounded, navigational)
3. Validation before CTA
4. FAQs answer the *doubt*, not just the question

### **Booking**
1. Remove friction language ("no forms," "no pressure," "24-hour response")
2. Warm up FAQs with permission language
3. Trust signals (95% move forward) with context, not numbers

## Common Pitfalls & Fixes

| **Pitfall** | **Why It Breaks Voice** | **Fix** |
|---|---|---|
| "Here are 5 tips to..." | Prescriptive; suggests quick fix | "You might notice... patterns like..." |
| "Despite the grief..." | Dismisses contradiction | "Grief and joy coexist. Complexity is human." |
| "You've got this!" | Performative; low substance | "That's real. Let's understand it together." |
| "Healing journey" | Vague; implies linear progress | "Integration," "reconstruction," "understanding" |
| "Don't worry about..." | Dismissive; sounds patronizing | "That doubt is honest. Let's explore it." |

## Tone Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Does this validate *and* name mechanism?
- [ ] Am I holding contradiction (both/and)?
- [ ] Is shame reframed (different, not broken)?
- [ ] Did I avoid prescriptive language (do this / don't do that)?
- [ ] Would Rainuka say this out loud to a client?
- [ ] Is there a short affirmation followed by longer explanation?
- [ ] Did I use permission language, not judgment?
- [ ] Zero clichés / pop-psychology jargon?

## Brand Voice in Action: Comparison

**WRONG:**
> "Don't let postpartum anxiety control you. Here are 5 coping strategies to manage it."
> *Why:* Prescriptive, minimizes, offers quick-fix.

**RIGHT:**
> "Postpartum anxiety is real. Your brain is adapting to massive change. There are patterns we can work with, tools that help you understand what's happening. You're not broken—your system is responding."
> *Why:* Validates, names mechanism, gives permission, reframes shame.
