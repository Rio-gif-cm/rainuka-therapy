# Rainuka Therapy Brand Voice Audit
**Date:** September 1, 2026  
**Audit Scope:** 5 Primary Pages (Homepage, Perinatal, ADHD, Career, Booking)  
**Consistency Score:** 8.5/10

---

## Executive Summary

**Finding:** Rainuka's voice is **highly consistent** across all primary pages. A distinct, personal therapeutic presence emerges—warm, direct, knowledgeable—that stays recognizably the same whether discussing perinatal loss, ADHD diagnosis, career burnout, or booking a consultation.

**Tone Signature:** "Seen and Understood" — the therapist who gets it, names what's hard without sugarcoating, and moves you toward clarity rather than judgment.

---

## Consistency Analysis by Page

### 1. **Homepage** (`app/page.tsx`)
**Voice Characteristics:**
- **Warmth:** High. Accessible, inviting, assumes clients are smart.
- **Tone:** Conversational, permission-giving ("You don't come to fix what's broken").
- **Language:** Practical, metaphor-rich ("find solid ground when everything feels tangled").
- **Pressure Level:** Low. Emphasizes "free consultation," "no commitment."

**Key Phrases:**
- "Therapy that fits the life you actually have"
- "You don't come to fix what's broken. You come to understand why you react the way you do."
- "Real tools that work today, not just insight."

**Vulnerability:** Present. Acknowledges complexity ("patterns you keep missing," "everything feels tangled").

### 2. **Perinatal Page** (`app/perinatal/page.tsx`)
**Voice Characteristics:**
- **Warmth:** Very high. Deeply validating, emotionally attuned.
- **Tone:** Nurtured, permission-giving, grief-aware.
- **Language:** Emotive, specific ("The invisible grief," "racing thoughts about baby safety").
- **Pressure Level:** Very low. Emphasis on "judgment-free," "no platitudes."

**Key Phrases:**
- "What you're carrying isn't weakness."
- "Judgment-free. Your pace. Anger, despair, hope—all belong here."
- "Love your baby and resent the loss of freedom. Feel relief after loss. Want connection but need solitude. All real, all true."
- "Validation, Not Platitudes"

**Vulnerability:** High. Normalizes contradictions, grief, rage.

**Consistency Note:** ✅ **Perfect alignment.** The voice is more emotionally present here because the topic requires it, but the core message (understanding > fixing, grief as valid) echoes the homepage.

### 3. **ADHD Page** (`app/adhd/page.tsx`)

- **Tone:** Validating shame, reframing deficit language ("different, not broken").
- **Language:** Concrete, strength-based, grief + relief dual-hold.
- **Pressure Level:** Low. Normalizes confusion and lost time.

**Key Phrases:**
- "Executive function difference—your brain works differently."
- "Different, not broken. We'll build on your strengths."
- "You've been surviving without the tools. Now you know. Everything changes."
- "Shame is inherited, not intrinsic."

**Vulnerability:** High. Directly addresses internalized criticism and identity reconstruction.

**Consistency Note:** ✅ **Strong alignment.** Tone shifts from perinatal's "grief of loss" to ADHD's "grief of late recognition," but the underlying message stays: "You're not broken; your system responded to unmet needs."

**Key Phrases:**

**Vulnerability:** Present but different. Acknowledges exhaustion, perfectionism traps, grief for paths not taken.

**Consistency Note:** ✅ **Very strong alignment.** Tone is more clinical/practical, but still centered on understanding over fixing. Same core belief: clarity comes from honest examination, not external answers.

## Tone Architecture: The "Rainuka Effect"

### Sentence Structure Signature
- **Pattern 1:** Short imperative/affirmation + longer explanation
  - "Your grief is real. Your anxiety is real. Both coexist with joy."
  - "Different, not broken. We'll build on your strengths."

- **Pattern 2:** Normalization via dual-hold (contradiction naming)
  - "You don't come to fix what's broken. You come to understand..."
  - "Love and space coexist. Grief and relief coexist."

- **Pattern 3:** Reframing shame/deficit language
  - "Executive function *difference*" (not disorder)
  - "That's *data*" (not failure)

### Vocabulary Signature
- **Favored Terms:** "real," "navigate," "process," "ground," "patterns," "clarify," "shame"
- **Avoided Terms:** "fix," "overcome," "manage," "cope," "resilience" (overused therapy speak)
- **Metaphor Preferences:** Body-based, navigation-based, grounding-based

### Emotional Tempo
- Pages flow: observation → validation → mechanism → permission → clarity
- No rushing to solutions
- Sits with contradiction

## Gaps & Opportunities for Consistency

### Minor Voice Inconsistency
1. **Booking page lacks personality.** Compared to specialty pages, it's more transactional. Opportunity: lean into "Let's Talk" tone more deeply; warm up FAQs.
2. **Career page is least emotionally present.** Makes sense (not grief-based), but could integrate more vulnerability language ("imposter syndrome is loud" is good; more like this).

### Recommended Consistency Tweaks
- **Homepage:** Could add one line about "your actual life" (career-specific). Currently assumes breadth; career section feels adjacent.
- **Booking:** Could mirror "Let's Talk" warmth in FAQ responses (currently generic).
- **All pages:** Maintain "both/and" framing (grief AND joy, shame-free AND understanding-based).

## Key Findings Summary

1. **Rainuka is present on every page.** Voice is unmistakably singular.
2. **No tone-shifting between topics.** Perinatal's warmth ≠ career's directness. Both are Rainuka.
3. **Core message is consistent:** Understand, don't fix. Validate, don't platitudinize. Clarify, don't prescribe.
4. **The "Rainuka Effect":** A therapist who removes shame, names mechanisms, and honors complexity.
5. **Warmth is intentional, not accidental.** Fluctuates with emotional load of topic, not writer.
