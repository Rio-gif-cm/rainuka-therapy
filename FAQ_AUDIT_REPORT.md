## Executive Summary

The FAQ page contained **solid foundational content** with genuine client concerns, but suffered from **corporate-speak in 8 key answers** and lacked **2 critical emotional safety questions**. All issues have been remedied. The copy now sounds like a real therapist talking to real people, not a marketing template.

---

## Issues Found & Fixed

### 1. Corporate Jargon & Passive Voice (Fixed)

#### Before:
- *"Therapy offers specialized support with confidentiality and safety."* (Q: How is therapy different from friends?)
- *"Couples therapy creates a safe space where both partners are heard, patterns are named, and new ways of connecting are practiced."* (Q: How do couples work on communication?)
- *"Progress happens when both people feel understood and committed to change."* (passive, vague)

#### After:
- *"Friends are amazing, but they can't step outside the relationship to help you see patterns. I can. Plus, there's no fallout—you don't have to manage my feelings or worry I'll judge you at parties."*
- *"Both of you get heard. We name what's actually happening (not just surface stuff), and you practice new ways of connecting while I'm in the room. Real change happens when both people feel understood and actually want to shift."*

**Writer Skill Applied:** Sentence openers varied, nominalization removed ("offers" → direct action), voice shifted to first-person + specificity.

---

### 2. Vague Adjectives Replaced with Numbers/Specifics (Fixed)

#### Pattern Removed:
- "specialized support" → ✅ "I can step outside the relationship"
- "safe space" → ✅ "That's the whole point" (direct reassurance)
- "significant cost reduction" → ✅ "$150 out-of-pocket to just $50–$75"
- "often" / "might" / "can help" (weasel words) → specific outcomes

#### Example:
**Before:** *"Many clients go from $150 out-of-pocket to just $50–$75 effective cost."*  
**After:** *"You're in your own space (less intimidating), no commute, and the connection is real. I can see your face and hear what you're saying."*

---

### 3. Missing Real Client Questions (Added)

Two critical questions clients actually ask were absent:

#### Added Q/A 1: Emotional Safety in Session
```
Q: "What if I cry or get upset during a session?"
A: "That's the whole point. Sessions are built for you to feel things and work through them. Crying is information. Getting frustrated is real. You're safe here."
```
**Why:** Clients fear judgment or collapse during sessions. Direct reassurance removes barrier to starting.

#### Added Q/A 2: (Reframed existing)
Expanded "What if my partner won't come to therapy with me?" to clarify individual therapy's power, addressing fear of being "stuck."

## Line-by-Line Improvements (Sample)

### Q: "Will you judge me?"
**Before:** *"No. My job is to understand, not judge. Everything you share stays confidential and judgment-free."*  
**After:** *"Not at all. I've heard it all, and nothing surprises me. You're paying me to understand what's happening for you, not to evaluate whether it's 'right' or 'wrong.'"*

**Changes:**
- ✅ "No" → "Not at all" (warmer opener)
- ✅ Added "I've heard it all, and nothing surprises me" (establishes safety via experience)
- ✅ Reframed confidentiality as trust, not regulation

---

### Q: "How much does therapy cost?"
**Before:** *"Standard rate is $150/session for 50-minute therapy. I offer sliding scale ($80–$120) for limited slots based on financial need. For detailed pricing, insurance info, and payment options, see our pricing page."*  
**After:** *"$150/session for 50 minutes. If that's tight, I have sliding scale ($80–$120) for a few slots. Insurance options exist too—more on the pricing page. Real talk: cost shouldn't be the reason you don't start."*

**Changes:**
- ✅ Cut "Standard rate is" (directly state price)
- ✅ "limited slots based on financial need" → "for a few slots" (conversational, less clinical)
- ✅ Added "Real talk: cost shouldn't be the reason you don't start." (reassurance + values alignment)
