# Brand Positioning Playbook
**Rainuka Therapy | 2026**

---

## The Rainuka Brand in One Line
**"Therapy for adults healing from invisible struggles—by someone who's lived them."**

---

## What Makes Rainuka Different
**Not:** "Warm, understanding therapist"  
**But:** **"Therapist with lived experience in perinatal mental health, late ADHD diagnosis, and career burnout"**

- She's not an outside expert observing your struggle
- She's someone who's sat in the same hard places
- She knows what it takes to move through them

---

## Core Niches (The Three That Define Her)
1. **Perinatal Mental Health** (anxiety, depression, grief, loss, postpartum struggles)
2. **Late ADHD Diagnosis** (grief + relief for adults diagnosed after 25, often women)
3. **Career Transitions & Burnout** (burnout, identity crisis, finding work that fits)

**Note:** These three niches connect Rainuka's actual lived story. We specialize, not generalize. Other therapists say "I work with anxiety/depression/life transitions." Rainuka's specificity is her differentiator.

---

## Brand Personality (The "Rainuka Effect")

### Tone Signature
- ✅ **Warm but direct.** Says hard truths without sugarcoating.
- ✅ **Grounded in specifics.** Not abstract wisdom; concrete, lived examples.
- ✅ **Permission-giving.** Removes shame through validation + mechanism-naming.
- ✅ **Non-prescriptive.** "Here's what I see. You decide."

### Core Beliefs
1. **Healing requires understanding, not willpower.** Your system is protecting you; let's understand why.
2. **Invisible struggles need naming.** Perinatal anxiety, late ADHD diagnosis, burnout—these are real conditions, not personal failings.
3. **You don't come to therapy to be fixed.** You come to understand yourself differently so you can move through patterns.
4. **Complexity is real.** You can grieve AND feel relief. Love your baby AND resent the loss of freedom. Both/and, not either/or.

### Vocabulary Signature

**Use These Words:**
- real, navigate, patterns, ground, both/and
- nervous system, data (not failure), moved through
- validate, mechanism, clarity, understand

**Avoid These Words:**
- resilience (overused therapy-speak)
- coping (minimizes struggle)
- overcome (implies weakness)
- manage (too clinical)
- whole person (jargon)
- "journey" (cliché)

## The Three Core Messages

### Message 1: Lived Experience (The Hook)
**For Homepage Hero / Primary CTA**
- *"Therapy by someone who's actually lived through perinatal anxiety, late ADHD diagnosis, and career burnout."*
- Why: Differentiates Rainuka from "generic warm therapist"
- Proof: About page, testimonials from people in these specific niches

### Message 2: Shame Reframing (The Promise)
**For Specialty Pages / Copy Throughout**
- *"You're not too much. The situation is."* (or niche-specific: *"Your perinatal anxiety doesn't mean you're failing"*)
- Why: Reframes the reader's self-blame
- Proof: Rainuka's clinical approach (understands nervous system, names mechanisms)

### Message 3: Understanding Over Fixing (The Philosophy)
**For About Page / Deeper Engagement**
- *"Healing isn't about willpower or positivity—it's about understanding why your nervous system is working this hard."*
- Why: Sets clear expectations for therapy approach
- Proof: Service descriptions, testimonials, case studies

## Visual Brand Personality

### Color
- **Warm, grounded, NOT corporate:** Think warm earth tones, not sterile whites
- **Current palette:** Burgundy (primary) + sage (secondary) + cream (neutral)
- **Feel:** Calming, professional, intimate—like a therapist's office you'd actually want to be in

### Imagery
- **Real people, not stock photos.** Diversity, vulnerability, strength
- **Avoid:** Clinical settings, fake smiling, inspirational poster vibes
- **Embrace:** Actual faces, real moments, people who look like Rainuka's clients

### Typography
- **Conversational, not formal.** Clear readability.
- **Avoid:** Script fonts, trendy typefaces
- **Use:** Warm sans-serif (readable) + serif (trustworthy) combo

## Messaging Hierarchy (Information Order)

**What visitors should know, in order:**

1. **Hero (Above fold):** "You're not too much. The situation is." + lived-experience subheading
2. **Proof (First scroll):** "I've lived it" callout with Rainuka's three core struggles
3. **For You (Secondary sections):** Three niche cards emphasizing Rainuka's lived experience + specific approach
4. **How It Works (Mid-page):** Booking process, what to expect (removes friction)
5. **Trust (Lower page):** Testimonials from people in core niches, credentials
6. **Final CTA:** "Start with someone who gets it" (emphasizes unique value, not just "book")

**Current Problem:** Lived-experience angle is buried in About page. Should be in hero or first scroll.

## Content Principles

### For Copy
- **Lead with the reader, not Rainuka.** "You're struggling with X" not "I specialize in X"
- **Be specific.** "Perinatal anxiety makes you catastrophize about baby safety" beats "I work with postpartum anxiety"
- **Use contractions.** "You're" not "You are." Conversational, not formal.
- **Name emotions.** "This is hard" not "This can be challenging"
- **Both/and framing.** Honor complexity: grief AND joy, shame-free AND understanding-based

### For Design
- **Hierarchy serves positioning.** Big visual weight on "I've lived it" angle
- **Whitespace is generous.** Not cramped; reflects the calm, grounded feeling
- **Imagery reinforces vulnerability.** Not stock photos of people in power poses
- **Color guides emotion.** Burgundy is warm (connection); sage is grounding (safety)

### For UX
- **Primary CTA reflects unique value.** Not just "Schedule Now"—something like "Start with Someone Who Gets It"
- **Form language removes barriers.** Not "Chief complaint" but "What's on your mind?"
- **Booking flow reassures.** "I'll reach out within 24 hours" (personal, not "automated response")
- **Navigation shows all three niches equally.** Perinatal, ADHD, Career are co-equal

## What Rainuka Is NOT

- ❌ Generic warm therapist
- ❌ Expert who observes from outside
- ❌ "Coaching" (she's clinical, not prescriptive)
- ❌ Offering quick fixes or "resilience hacks"
- ❌ Corporate or clinical-distance
- ❌ One-size-fits-all ("I work with everything")

---

## What Rainuka IS

- ✅ Therapist with lived experience
- ✅ Someone who's sat in the hard places
- ✅ Specific about her niches (not generalist)
- ✅ Direct + warm (not saccharine)
- ✅ Understanding-focused (not fixing-focused)
- ✅ Grounded in her own story

## Quick Checks Before Publishing

**Does this copy/design/feature pass the Rainuka test?**

- [ ] Does it emphasize lived experience or just warmth?
- [ ] Is it specific (names actual struggles) or generic?
- [ ] Would Rainuka actually say this in a session?
- [ ] Does it sound like *one person*, not a team?
- [ ] Does it honor complexity (both/and) or oversimplify?
- [ ] Is it permission-giving, not prescriptive?

**If you answer "no" to any of these, revise before commit.**
