## CONCERNS & TRADE-OFFS

### Potential Objection: "Won't longer copy hurt conversion?"
**Agent C Response:** The proposed changes *aren't* longer—they're more specific. They replace multiple vague adjectives with one concrete sentence. Example:
- Old: "compassionate, trauma-informed, culturally responsive support" (3 adjectives)
- New: "Someone who listens without rushing to solutions. Who validates your experience before offering tools." (1 specific paragraph, but stronger signal)

### Potential Objection: "What if clients expect 'safe space' language?"
**Agent C Response:** They do—but "safe space" is a red flag for corporate therapy speak. Our differentiation is *specificity*. Better to educate them on what we actually offer (understanding, concrete tools, honest relationship) than reinforce industry boilerplate.
