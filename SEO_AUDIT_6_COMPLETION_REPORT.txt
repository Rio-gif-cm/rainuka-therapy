## KEY INSIGHTS

### What's Working
✅ **Website foundation is solid**
- Clean, semantic HTML structure
- Navigation properly organized
- Multiple specialty pages with good content depth
- Existing testimonials and social proof
- Mobile-responsive design

### What's Missing
❌ **Authority signals**
- No external backlink profile at all
- No verification of therapist credentials by external sources
- No professional association links
- No E-E-A-T signals

❌ **Internal architecture**
- No hub-spoke topology for link juice distribution
- Specialty pages siloed (don't link to each other)
- No hub pages centralizing authority
- Missing contextual linking opportunities

❌ **Backlink strategy**
- No systematic link acquisition plan
- No guest post strategy
- No directory placement roadmap
- No local SEO foundation

### Why This Matters
**Therapy is a YMYL (Your Money, Your Life) category.** Google applies stricter ranking criteria:
- E-E-A-T (Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) signals are CRITICAL
- External authority links are weighted 3-5x heavier than average niches
- Medical/mental health sites need verified credentials and professional association backing

**Current problem:** Rainuka's therapy site has NO external authority signals, making it nearly invisible to Google for competitive keywords despite good on-page optimization.

**The fix:** Every recommendation in this audit is designed to build external authority systematically.
