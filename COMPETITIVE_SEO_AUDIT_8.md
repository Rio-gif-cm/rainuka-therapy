## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Rainuka operates in one of the most crowded, directory-dominated therapy markets. The competitive landscape has shifted fundamentally in 2026: **Psychology Today's dominance is eroding, AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) are reshaping discovery, and individual therapist websites are now the single most-cited touchpoint (90% of AI answers cite at least one practice website).**

Rainuka's site has solid foundational structure but **is invisible in the competitive set where it matters most**: AI search recommendations, keyword rankings, backlink authority, and content depth vs. competitors.

**Key Finding:** Rainuka ranks well for zero primary keywords where competitors dominate. Psychology Today profiles, TherapyDen listings, and locally-optimized competitor websites receive 75%+ of discovery traffic. Rainuka's site has zero citations in AI search results despite strong content foundations.

- **90% cited at least one individual practice website** — but only the top-ranked ones
- **Psychology Today appeared in 52 citations** (dominant)
- **Zencare: 5/5 appearances in AI Brand Tracker queries**
- **TherapyDen: 5/5 appearances in AI Brand Tracker queries**
- **Rainuka: 0/96 mentions**

### Why Rainuka Doesn't Appear in AI Answers

**AI search visibility requires:**
1. ✅ Structured data (MedicalBusiness schema) — Rainuka has this
2. ❌ Multiple pages on the same topic — Rainuka has 1-2 pages per specialty
3. ❌ Backlink authority — Rainuka has 0-5 backlinks
4. ❌ Consistent NAP across directories — Rainuka is not listed on major directories
5. ❌ FAQ schema on service pages — Rainuka has FAQs but they may not be properly marked
6. ❌ Content that answers real questions — Rainuka's content exists but is not discoverable

**Immediate AI search improvement:** Add Rainuka to TherapyDen, GoodTherapy, and Zencare (free or low-cost). Competitors who appear in 3-5 directories get cited in AI results.

**Long-term AI search improvement:** 20-30 blog posts + 5-10 dedicated specialty pages = enough content depth for AI systems to cite reliably.

**Critical Gap #6:** Rainuka is completely absent from AI search despite having the foundational structure needed to appear.

## 10. SPEED & PERFORMANCE WINS (RAINUKA'S ONLY EDGE)

Rainuka's site loads in 1.8s (LCP), beating Psychology Today (2.8s), BetterHelp (2.1s), and most competitors. However:

- **Speed doesn't rank keywords.** Keywords rank from content + authority + backlinks.

**Action:** Keep speed optimized, but prioritize content + authority — speed alone is not a competitive advantage in 2026.
