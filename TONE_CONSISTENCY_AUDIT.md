## Improvements Made

### 1. LGBTQ+ Page (`/lgbtq`)
**Key Changes:**
- **"What Affirming Means"** section: Added explicit rejection of subtle gatekeeping ("There is no 'but let's work on' hiding underneath my affirmation")
- **Core Commitment:** Shifted from abstract to concrete ("celebrates who you are")
- **Gender-Affirming Care:** Added "fully" and clarified autonomy language, addressed disguised clinical delays
- **Intersectionality card:** Strengthened to emphasize each identity matters independently + how they interact

**Before:**
> "Real affirmation of your identity, your relationships, and your lived experience—held as fact, not as a topic up for debate."

**After:**
> "Not a therapist who tolerates you like you're doing them a favor. Real affirmation of your identity, your relationships, and your lived experience—held as simple fact, not as a topic up for debate or therapy to resolve."

---

### 2. BIPOC Page (`/bipoc`)
**Key Changes:**
- **Anti-Racism Therapy intro:** Expanded "I don't see race" to explicitly call out the harm of colorblindness
- **Core Commitment:** Changed "Your anger at injustice is not a pathology" to "is a rational response to real harm" (validation, not just normalization)
- Added **"You won't have to explain racism to me"** — direct, warm, specific promise
- **Cultural Humility card:** Emphasize "You are the expert on your own life" + openness ("I won't pretend I understand")

**Before:**
> "Your anger at injustice is not a pathology. Your caution in unsafe spaces is wisdom, not paranoia. We name oppression directly instead of treating it as background noise in your own story."

**After:**
> "Your anger at injustice is not a pathology to manage—it's a rational response to real harm. Your caution in unsafe spaces is wisdom, not paranoia or defensiveness. We name oppression directly and clearly, not as background noise in your own story. You won't have to explain racism to me. We can focus on how to survive it and thrive anyway."

---

### 3. Neurodivergent Page (`/neurodivergent`)
**Key Changes:**
- **What Affirming Means:** Shifted from abstract "Not compliance" to concrete example of harm ("teaching you to mask more convincingly")
- **Core Commitment:** Added "You've been trying to be normal for long enough. Let's build something that actually works." — Validating, practical
- **Strengths-based card:** Expanded to include "deep integrity" and "fierce loyalty" alongside ADHD/autistic traits, reframed as not "compensations"
- **Identity-first language:** Clarified why ("shapes how you think, relate, and move through the world")
- **Sensory-informed care:** Reframed from accommodation to "central to understanding you"

**Before (Core Commitment):**
> "We work from the neurodiversity paradigm: ADHD and autism are natural variations in how brains work, not defects to be cured or normalized. The goal is a life that fits you—not a you that fits someone else's life."

**After:**
> "We work from the neurodiversity paradigm: ADHD and autism are natural variations in how brains work, not defects to be cured or 'corrected' so you fit the mold. The goal is a life that fits you—not a you that fits someone else's expectations. You've been trying to be normal for long enough. Let's build something that actually works."

## Specific Tone Shifts Applied

### 1. **Removed Clinical Distance**
- ❌ "Your neurodivergence is not a disorder to overcome"
- ✅ "You've been trying to be normal for long enough. Let's build something that actually works."

### 2. **Added Validation of Lived Experience**
- ❌ "Your anger at injustice is not a pathology"
- ✅ "Your anger at injustice is not a pathology to manage—it's a rational response to real harm"

### 3. **Reframed Affirmation as Active, Not Passive**
- ❌ "Real affirmation of your identity and your lived experience"
- ✅ "Real affirmation of your identity and your lived experience—held as simple fact, not as a topic up for debate or therapy to resolve" + "celebrates who you are"

### 4. **Made Promises Specific & Warm**
- ❌ "Cultural humility means I stay curious"
- ✅ "You won't have to explain racism to me. We can focus on how to survive it and thrive anyway."

### 5. **Strengthened Strengths-Based Language**
- ❌ "Your neurodivergence comes with real gifts"
- ✅ "ADHD hyperfocus, autistic pattern recognition, deep integrity, fierce loyalty, creative problem-solving—your neurodivergence comes with real gifts that aren't just 'managing compensations.' We explore what you are genuinely good at"
