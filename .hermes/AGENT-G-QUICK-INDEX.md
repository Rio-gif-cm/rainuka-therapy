## 🎯 THE 3 CONCEPTS: Quick Summary

### Concept 1: "What Therapy Actually Feels Like"
- **Problem it solves:** Client anxiety about first session ("What will actually happen?")
- **Format:** 5-stage interactive walkthrough (timeline or accordion)
- **Length:** ~3-4 minute read
- **Difficulty:** Medium (1-2 weeks)
- **SEO value:** High (long-tail keywords: "what is therapy like", "first session")
- **Location:** Dedicated page or About/Services section
- **Status:** Outlined, ready for design + content refinement

### Concept 2: "Meet Me Where You Are"
- **Problem it solves:** Emotional connection ("Does she actually get me?")
- **Format:** 2-3 min unscripted video (Rainuka's story)
- **Coverage:** Perinatal (infertility), ADHD (invisible chaos), Career (burnout)
- **Difficulty:** Easy (1 week)
- **SEO value:** Medium (referral driver more than organic search)
- **Location:** Homepage hero OR dedicated /meet-rainuka page
- **Status:** Outlined, ready for video production + script refinement
- **Recommendation:** LAUNCH FIRST (highest impact, fastest to produce)

### Concept 3: "Things Clients Ask"
- **Problem it solves:** Shame isolation ("Am I the only one?")
- **Format:** 10-50 Q&A cards (expandable, searchable)
- **Covers:** First session fear, crying, cost, meds, vulnerability, burnout, sex, family
- **Difficulty:** Easy (scalable start with 10)
- **SEO value:** High (long-tail keywords for each question)
- **Location:** Dedicated page OR inline throughout site
- **Status:** Outlined with 7 sample Q&As, ready for expansion + design

## 🎬 RECOMMENDED LAUNCH SEQUENCE

**Week 1-2: Video** (Concept 2)

**Week 4+: Q&A** (Concept 3)
- Continuous expansion
- Grows with real client questions
