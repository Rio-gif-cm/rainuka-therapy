## Key Insights I'm Flagging

### Insight #1: "The Unasked Question"
**The biggest gap:** Rainuka's boundaries + limitations

Current content is strong on warmth, weak on clarity about:
- "Will you tell me what to do?"
- "Will you fix me?"
- "Can I text anytime?"
- "Do you have to report me if...?"

**Why it matters:** Anxious clients need to know BOTH what you'll do AND what you won't. Explicitly stating boundaries actually increases trust.

**My recommendation:** Add "What I Won't Do" section to Concept 1 + "My Limitations" Q&A to Concept 3.
