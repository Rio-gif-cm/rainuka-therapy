## Key Strategic Insights

### INSIGHT #1: The "Unasked Question" Problem
**Current state:** 3 concepts are strong on emotional warmth, weak on boundary clarity

**What anxious clients actually need to know:**
- "Will you tell me what to do?" (No—you decide)
- "Will you fix me?" (No—you heal yourself with support)
- "Can I text anytime?" (No—email works; crisis calls, not texts)
- "Do I have to talk about my childhood?" (Only if relevant)
- "What if we don't click?" (Easy to switch)

**Solution:** Added "What I Won't Do" section to Concept 1 + "My Limitations" Q&A

**Why it works:** Explicitly naming boundaries paradoxically increases trust. Clients relax when they know the rules.
