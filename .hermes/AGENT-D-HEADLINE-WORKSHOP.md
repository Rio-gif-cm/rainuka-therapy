**Current Headline**: "You don't have to carry this alone"

---

## Workshop Goals

1. **Move beyond corporate language** — No "Therapy for X" or clinical positioning
2. **Anchor in real emotional specifics** — Not vague, not inspirational-poster vibes
3. **Authentic voice** — Match Rainuka's warmth + expertise, not marketing-speak
4. **Narrative hooks** — Each headline opens a conversation, not closes with certainty

---

## 5 Headline Variations

### OPTION 1: "Perinatal anxiety doesn't mean you're failing"
**Vibe**: Reframe shame into normal response  
**Emotional hook**: Directly confronts the lie that perinatal anxiety is a personal failure  
**Why it works**:
- Specific (names the audience experience explicitly)
- Permission-giving (subtext: "this is understandable, treatable, you're not broken")
- Conversational tone ("doesn't mean")
- Works for all three niches: perinatal shame, ADHD shame, career doubt shame

**Subheading support**: "Postpartum anxiety, ADHD diagnosis, career transitions—these aren't reflections of who you are. They're things we work through together."

---

### OPTION 2: "Your late ADHD diagnosis wasn't hiding until now"
**Vibe**: Normalize masking + reframe as adaptation  
**Emotional hook**: Acknowledges the shock of late diagnosis + validates all the years before  
**Why it works**:
- Speaks to lived experience (late diagnosis is unexpected + often brings guilt)
- Shifts narrative from "you missed something" to "you've been adapting all along"
- Specific enough to catch ADHD folks immediately; warm enough to extend to perinatal/career

**Subheading support**: "You didn't just wake up with ADHD. You've been managing it. Let's work with what that means for your life now."

---

### OPTION 3: "Burnout doesn't fix itself, but you don't have to figure it out alone"
**Vibe**: Validates the exhaustion, offers partnership not heroics  
**Emotional hook**: Acknowledges both the reality (burnout won't disappear) and the relief (you have support)  
**Why it works**:
- Concrete + honest (no false promise of quick fix)
- Emphasizes partnership, not expert-fixing-you
- Reaches career people, perinatal folks, ADHD burnout all at once
- Direct address to exhaustion ("figure it out")

**Subheading support**: "Career doubt. Anxiety in pregnancy. ADHD late diagnosis. These aren't problems you solve alone on a weekend. They're things we work through."

---

### OPTION 4: "What if the panic isn't a problem to fix, but something to understand?"
**Vibe**: Reframe anxiety from threat to information  
**Emotional hook**: Shifts the frame from "fix me" to "help me see what this means"  
**Why it works**:
- Specific to anxiety (perinatal panic, ADHD overwhelm, career anxiety)
- Philosophical without being abstract (grounded in therapy principle)
- Opens curiosity instead of shame
- Invites collaboration ("what if we looked at this together?")

**Subheadline support**: "Perinatal anxiety, late ADHD diagnosis, career transitions—each of these brings panic. Let's figure out what it's telling you."

---

### OPTION 5: "You're not too much. The situation is."
**Vibe**: Direct reframe of core shame narrative  
**Emotional hook**: Names the internalized shame directly, offers permission  
**Why it works**:
- Short, punchy, memorable
- Hits the core shame narrative (especially for ADHD/perinatal/career-doubt folks)
- Validates the person, not the symptom
- Warm + direct (matches Rainuka's voice in testimonials)
- Works across all three niches

**Subheading support**: "Perinatal anxiety doesn't mean you're overwhelmed as a parent. ADHD doesn't mean you're broken at work. Career doubt doesn't mean you're failing. It means the situation needs rethinking."
