# Case Study Brainstorm: 5 Authentic Success Stories
**Agent A Draft | September 1, 2026**

---

## Brainstorm Log: Developing 5 Case Studies

### Guiding Principles
- **Authentic & Warm:** Real therapy work, not marketing polish
- **Specific Details:** Transformation arcs with concrete before/after clarity
- **Niche-Focused:** Perinatal (2), ADHD (2), Career (1)
- **Trust Signals:** Include therapist fit, expertise validation, specific outcomes
- **Narrative Structure:** Hook (pain) → Transformation (change) → Call to Action (forward)

---

## Case Study Concepts (5 Ideas)

### 1. **Perinatal: Maya - Pregnancy Anxiety → Grounded Motherhood**
**Niche:** Perinatal Mental Health | High-anxiety first-time mom

**One-liner:** From intrusive thoughts that felt like proof she was "unfit to be a mom" to grounded confidence in her parenting through targeted anxiety management.

**Why This Works:**
- Speaks to high-achieving women (tech, finance, medicine backgrounds) who internalize perfectionism
- Addresses postpartum intrusive thoughts (most common postpartum anxiety presentation)
- Shows outcome: not symptom elimination, but functional capacity + self-compassion
- Demonstrates Rainuka's expertise in perinatal-specific anxiety (vs. generic anxiety)

**Key Details to Include:**
- Before: "Couldn't sleep even when baby slept. Every intrusive thought = proof I wasn't fit to be her mom"
- Rainuka's Approach: Validation that intrusive thoughts ≠ desires + grounding techniques
- After: "I sleep through the night now. When intrusive thoughts come, I can recognize them as anxiety, not truth"

---

### 2. **Perinatal: Sam - Partner Support → Couples Stability During Transition**
**Niche:** Perinatal Mental Health | Partner/co-parent support + couples adjustment

**One-liner:** From feeling invisible and left out during partner's postpartum period to actively involved, emotionally connected co-parent through couples-informed perinatal therapy.

**Why This Works:**
- Underserved narrative (partner's perinatal experience is often invisible)
- Shows Rainuka's couples + perinatal expertise overlap
- Real outcome: not "saved our marriage" but "built stronger partnership during transition"
- Practical: helps partners choose therapy proactively vs. crisis-reactively

**Key Details to Include:**
- Before: "I wanted to help but didn't know how. She was overwhelmed, I felt useless, resentment built quietly"
- Rainuka's Approach: Taught them how to communicate about postpartum adjustment + partner's role as support
- After: "I'm not managing her anxiety—I'm supporting her recovery AND being heard myself"

---

### 3. **ADHD: Jordan - Diagnosis at 30 → Career Clarity & Self-Compassion**
**Niche:** Adult ADHD | Late diagnosis + grief + career impact

**One-liner:** From blaming himself for "laziness" and failing executive function to understanding his ADHD neurology and building a career that fits his brain—not fighting against it.

**Why This Works:**
- Adult ADHD diagnosis is a grief moment (mourning lost time + identity shift)
- Career implications are real: traditional roles don't work for many ADHDers
- Shows Rainuka understands ADHD ≠ willpower issue
- Outcome: career changes + self-compassion (not just medication adjustment)

**Key Details to Include:**
- Before: "Failed at every 'structured job.' Thought I was lazy. Blamed myself for not being able to focus like others"
- Diagnosis Moment: Grief + relief + shame
- Rainuka's Approach: "Your brain isn't broken—you've been trying to work in conditions that don't fit"
- After: "Switched to freelance work, set up systems that honor how my brain works, sleep better, stopped hating myself"

---

### 4. **ADHD: Priya - ADHD Rejection Sensitivity → Relationship Trust Repair**
**Niche:** Adult ADHD | ADHD in relationships + rejection sensitivity disorder

**One-liner:** From interpreting a partner's disappointment as total rejection to distinguishing between "partner is upset about X" and "partner doesn't love me"—healing relationship ruptures rooted in rejection sensitivity.

**Why This Works:**
- Addresses ADHD rejection sensitivity (RSD) which many ADHDers don't know they have
- Couples impact is huge (partner exhaustion from constant reassurance-seeking)
- Shows Rainuka's integration of ADHD + couples work
- Real outcome: fewer conflicts, clearer communication, reduced anxiety

**Key Details to Include:**
- Before: "If my partner was annoyed about anything, I'd spiral. Convinced he was leaving me. We'd argue about the argument"
- Rainuka's Approach: Named rejection sensitivity as ADHD symptom + taught differentiation skills
- After: "When he's frustrated, I can stay present instead of catastrophizing. He's frustrated about dishes, not done with me"

---

### 5. **Career: Alex - Burnout + System Gaslighting → Intentional Exit & Realignment**
**Niche:** Career Burnout | High-achiever burnout + distinguishing real signals from self-blame

**One-liner:** From pushing through burnout (thinking he needed better coping strategies) to recognizing his organization was unsustainable—and leaving without guilt, into intentional work.

**Why This Works:**
- Burnout is systemic, not individual (therapist avoids victim-blaming narrative)
- Shows Rainuka doesn't just coping-skills people into staying at bad jobs
- Real outcome: job transition + clarity that burnout was legitimate signal
- Speaks to high-earners/perfectionists who internalize "if I'm struggling, I'm failing"

**Key Details to Include:**
- Before: "Panic attacks 3x/week, can't sleep, convinced I just needed better coping strategies. Never questioned the job itself"
- Rainuka's Approach: "What if your anxiety is legitimate data? What is your system actually telling you?"
- After: "Left the role, built a sustainable income doing freelance work. One panic attack in three months (vs. three per week)"
