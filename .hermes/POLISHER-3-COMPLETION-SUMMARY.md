## KEY FINDINGS (28 Issues Prioritized)

### 🔴 CRITICAL ISSUES (Blocking Local SEO)

1. **No Google Business Profile** (-60% local visibility)
   - Required for local search visibility, Maps integration, reviews
   - Takes 2-6 weeks to verify (phone call or postcard)
   - ACTION: Rainuka claims + verifies GBP immediately

2. **Business Name Chaos** (Entity fragmentation)
   - "Wonderloud Therapy" vs. "Rainuka Therapy" across different pages
   - Google treats as 2+ separate businesses
   - ACTION: Pick canonical "Rainuka Therapy", standardize everywhere

3. **Fake Placeholder Contact Info** (Cannot contact therapist)
   - Phone: +1-555-123-4567 (obviously fake)
   - Email inconsistency: contact@rainuka-therapy.com vs. contact@rainukatherapy.com
   - ACTION: Collect real phone + email from Rainuka, update everywhere

4. **Zero Directory Citations** (-30-40% ranking signals)
   - No Psychology Today, TherapyDen, GoodTherapy, Yelp profiles
   - Citations are 20-25% of local ranking algorithm
   - ACTION: Claim Tier 1 directories (Psychology Today, TherapyDen, GoodTherapy)

5. **No Review Infrastructure** (-25-40% CTR loss vs. competitors)
   - No Google reviews (after GBP created)
   - No automated review collection
   - Only internal testimonials (not third-party verified)
   - ACTION: Set up review request automation post-GBP

### 🟠 HIGH-PRIORITY ISSUES (40% Traffic Opportunity)

6. **No Location-Specific Pages** (-40-60% local search traffic)
   - Missing: /therapy-toronto, /therapy-california, /therapy-florida
   - High commercial intent keywords untargeted
   - ACTION: Create 3 primary + 5 secondary location pages

7. **Incomplete LocalBusiness Schema**
   - Missing: businessHours, geo, images, sameAs, aggregateRating link
   - Reduces Google's understanding of business
   - ACTION: Expand schema with all fields from template

8. **Insufficient Local Keywords** (-40% visibility for "near me" searches)
   - Homepage keywords lack geo modifiers
   - No "therapy Toronto," "ADHD in California," etc.
   - ACTION: Update meta descriptions + H1s with geo keywords

9. **Telehealth Not Optimized** (-20-30% of telehealth search volume)
   - "Online therapist," "virtual therapy," "telehealth [specialty]" untargeted
   - ACTION: Create /telehealth-therapy page + geo-specific telehealth content

### 🟡 MEDIUM-PRIORITY ISSUES

10. **Missing Testimonial Consent** (Legal risk)
    - No clear written consent on display testimonials
    - HIPAA compliance unclear
    - ACTION: Audit testimonials, create consent form, document permissions
