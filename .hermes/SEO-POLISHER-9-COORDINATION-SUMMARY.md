### 1. ✅ Synthesized All Auditor Feedback
**Source:** SEO Auditor #2 (On-Page Specialist)

**Key Findings Analyzed:**

- **Title Tags:** 25+ pages need shortening (45-88 chars → 50-60 char target)
- **Meta Descriptions:** 15+ pages missing CTAs or truncated (200+ chars)
- **H1 Tags:** 5+ pages missing H1s or lacking keyword matches
- **H2/H3 Hierarchy:** Header skipping on Perinatal, ADHD, Career pages

- **Breadcrumbs:** No visual breadcrumb navigation on nested pages

**Impact Assessment:**
- CTR Loss: -8-12% (truncated titles/descriptions in SERPs)
- Ranking Loss: -10-15% (weak keyword signals, poor hierarchy)
- Trust Loss: -5-10% (missing schema, no rich snippets)
