## 💡 WORDSMITHING PRIORITIES

When rewriting, optimize for:
1. **Specificity** (concrete details, not generalizations)
2. **Warmth** (genuine care, no clinical distance)
3. **Brevity** (respect readers' time; if it's long, it earns that length)
4. **Permission** (explicitly giving people permission to feel hard things)
5. **Clarity** (no jargon without translation)
