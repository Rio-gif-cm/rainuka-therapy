# CRITIC #8: RUTHLESS CONTENT DEPTH & COMPLETENESS AUDIT
**Rainuka Therapy Website | September 1, 2026**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This website has **excellent copywriting and strong emotional resonance**, but suffers from **critical content gaps, shallow specialty depth, and poor answer-ability on real client questions**. While pages are visually well-structured and tonally consistent, the actual *substance* is thin. Specialty pages are marketing-forward (good positioning, weak clinical depth). Critical resources are missing, incomplete, or underbaked. The site positions itself as specialized but doesn't deliver the depth that justifies those claims.

**Overall verdict**: Polished but hollow. Fix the 20+ problems below before claiming expertise.

---

## 20+ CONTENT DEPTH & COMPLETENESS PROBLEMS

### **1. SPECIALTY PAGES ARE THIN ON CLINICAL CONTENT**

**Problem**: Specialty pages (ADHD, Perinatal, Career, Couples, Grief, Neurodivergent) lean heavily on **marketing framing** ("Different, not broken," "Shame-aware") but provide minimal **clinical depth**. 

- **ADHD page** lists symptoms surface-level (time blindness, task initiation, emotional regulation) but never explains the *neurobiological mechanisms* (prefrontal cortex underdevelopment in ADHD, dopamine dysregulation, executive function networks). No reference to ADHD-specific research or assessment approaches.
- **Couples therapy page** mentions Gottman patterns (criticism, contempt, defensiveness, stonewalling) but doesn't explain *why* these patterns form, attachment-system roots, or how they're actually *treated* in session.
- **Grief page** categorizes loss types (bereavement, miscarriage, pet loss, ambiguous loss) but provides NO evidence-based frameworks (Dual Process Model, Meaning Reconstruction Theory, Worden's tasks of mourning).
- **Career page** talks about burnout vs. misalignment but never defines these clinically or references burnout assessment tools (Maslach Burnout Inventory, job demands-resources theory).

**Client impact**: Someone researching "what is ADHD therapy actually like" leaves without understanding treatment modalities (CBT, DBT, somatic work, medication-therapy integration). They get a *feeling*, not *competence*.

**Severity**: HIGH. This is existential—specialty pages should demonstrate clinical authority, not just emotional resonance.

### **2. MISSING TREATMENT MODALITIES & THEORETICAL FRAMEWORKS**

**Problem**: The site mentions **approach language** ("trauma-informed," "culturally humble," "practical") but never explains the *actual modalities* used in sessions.

- No mention of specific approaches: CBT, DBT, ACT, IFS, EMDR, somatic experiencing, psychodynamic, attachment-based therapy, etc.
- Perinatal page lists modalities briefly ("Trauma-focused CBT, Somatic approaches, Attachment-informed therapy, Mindfulness") but never explains what each *does* or when it's used.
- Career page never mentions any therapeutic model (cognitive reframing, motivational interviewing, narrative therapy, etc.).
- Couples page references Gottman but doesn't name the actual therapy model being used (Emotionally Focused Therapy? Gottman Method specifically?).
- **Client question**: "Do you use EMDR for trauma?" No answer on the site.

**Severity**: HIGH. Clients want to know *how* you work, not just *that* you work.

**Client question they don't answer**: "I think I have ADHD. How do I know for sure? Can you assess me or do I need to see someone else first?"

### **5. GRIEF PAGE IS THEORETICALLY SHALLOW**

**Problem**: The grief page lists loss types and timeline, but **no grief models or integration frameworks**.

- Mentions the myth of "five stages" (good debunking) but **never replaces it** with Worden's four tasks, Neimeyer's meaning reconstruction, or the Dual Process Model.
- Doesn't explain **ambiguous loss** clearly (Pauline Boss's framework—missing entirely).
- No mention of **complicated grief** vs. normal grief (when therapy is crucial).
- No explanation of how different attachment styles influence grief (preoccupied attachment = intense grief; dismissive = delayed grief, etc.).
- Timeline section is useful but doesn't account for **grief trauma** (sudden loss) vs. **anticipatory grief** vs. **disenfranchised grief** (different timelines).
- **Missing**: When to refer to grief-specific modalities (narrative therapy, Internal Family Systems for grief parts, etc.).

**Client impact**: Someone reading this knows grief is normal but leaves without understanding their *own* grief pattern or what therapy can actually *do* to help.

**Severity**: MEDIUM-HIGH. Grief is complex; the page simplifies beyond usefulness.

## FINAL THOUGHTS

**Strengths**:
- Copywriting is emotionally intelligent and relatable
- Trauma-informed language is consistent
- Visual design is clean and accessible
- Personal story in About is compelling and differentiating

**Weaknesses**:
- **Substance gap**: Feels like great marketing for a therapist who hasn't yet defined her actual clinical approach
- **Safety gaps**: Missing crisis protocol, abuse screening, diagnostic guidance
- **Authority gaps**: Credentials buried, research uncited, no external verification
- **Operational gaps**: Unclear booking/intake process, placeholder contact info, missing policies

**The core issue**: This site tells people *why* to choose Rainuka (relatability, trauma-informed, cultural humility) but not **how** she actually works or **what** to expect from therapy with her.

**Verdict**: Fix the critical and high-priority items before claiming expertise in your specializations. Right now, the site reads as "relatable, but am I actually qualified?" That's the opposite of the intended message.
