## 📋 THE 3 CONCEPTS

### **Concept 1: "What Therapy Actually Feels Like"**
- **Purpose:** Demystify first therapy session (reduce fear, build trust)
- **Format:** 5-stage interactive walkthrough showing Rainuka's actual listening process
- **Covers:** First 10 min → Story → Listening → Reframe → Closing
- **Difficulty:** Medium | **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks
- **Why it works:** Transparency + method = trust signal
- **Placement:** Dedicated page or About/Services section
- **SEO:** High (targets "what is therapy like" long-tail keywords)

### **Concept 2: "Meet Me Where You Are"** ⭐ RECOMMEND FIRST
- **Purpose:** Build emotional connection via vulnerability & presence
- **Format:** 2-3 minute unscripted video (Rainuka tells her story)
- **Covers:** Perinatal (infertility grief), ADHD (invisible chaos), Career (burnout recovery)
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Timeline:** 1 week
- **Why it works:** Video builds trust faster; shows real person, not authority
- **Placement:** Homepage hero (every visitor sees it)
- **Impact:** Referrals increase (therapists share with clients); highest emotional ROI

### **Concept 3: "Things Clients Ask"**
- **Purpose:** Give permission for shame-laden questions clients Google at midnight
- **Format:** 10-50 expandable Q&A cards (searchable, growing over time)
- **Covers:** Intrusive thoughts, ADHD shame, crying, costs, medication, vulnerability, burnout, sex/trauma, family conflict
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Timeline:** Scalable (start with 10, grow to 50)
- **Why it works:** Real questions in Rainuka's actual voice addressing the unspoken
- **Placement:** Dedicated page or inline throughout site
- **SEO:** High (long-tail keywords for each question)
- **Growth:** Expandable over time as real questions come in
