## 🎯 Quick Summary

### The Problem
Rainuka's brand is warm but **generic**. Competitors claim similar warmth. The unique angle—**she's lived through infertility, late ADHD diagnosis, career burnout**—is buried mid-page.

### The Solution
1. **Front-load lived-experience positioning** on homepage hero
2. **Adopt sharper headline:** "You're not too much. The situation is."
3. **Specialize in 3 core niches** (perinatal, ADHD, career) instead of generalist
4. **Develop distinctive personality** via POV + vocabulary signature
5. **Create visual/UX hierarchy** supporting the new positioning

### The Outcome (By Wednesday)
- ✅ Revised homepage headline + subheading
- ✅ Updated primary pages emphasizing lived experience
- ✅ Brand positioning statement defined
- ✅ Voice playbook circulated to all teams
- ✅ All disciplines aligned + ready to build
