## What I Found

### Strength ✅
**Voice consistency is solid (8.5/10).** Rainuka's tone is recognizably singular across all pages. Readers feel like they know the same person on homepage, specialty pages, booking page. This is a strong foundation.

### Weakness 🚨
**Differentiation gap:** The warm, understanding therapist brand is table stakes in therapy marketing. Many competitors claim similar warmth. **Rainuka's actual differentiator—lived experience with infertility, late ADHD diagnosis, career burnout—is buried mid-page.** Visitors don't immediately know she's speaking from lived experience, only that she's "warm and understanding."

### Opportunity 💡
**Front-load the lived-experience angle.** Move it from About page → Homepage hero. This creates immediate differentiation: not "generic warm therapist," but "therapist who's actually lived through your invisible struggle." This alone repositions the entire brand.
