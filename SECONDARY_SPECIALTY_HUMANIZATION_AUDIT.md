## Audit Methodology

Applied 34 AI writing patterns from the Humanizer skill (Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing) plus Writer skill patterns:

### Patterns Checked
1. Undue significance inflation (testament, pivotal, crucial, underscores)
2. Undue emphasis on notability/media coverage
3. Superficial -ing phrases (highlighting, underscoring, reflecting, contributing)
4. Promotional/advertisement language (boasts, vibrant, profound, nestled)
5. Vague attributions (Industry reports, Experts argue)
6. Outline-like "Challenges" sections
7. AI vocabulary overuse (key, landscape, showcase, essential)
8. Copula avoidance (serves as, stands as, marks, represents)
9. Negative parallelisms (It's not just X, it's Y)
10. Rule of three overuse
11. Elegant variation/synonym cycling
12. False ranges (from X to Y)
13. Passive voice problems
14. Em-dash overuse
15. Overuse of boldface
16. Emojis (mechanical decoration)
17. Curly quotation marks (ChatGPT tell)
18-22. Communication artifact patterns (here's, I hope this helps)
23-25. Filler, hedging, generic conclusions
26. Hyphenated word pair overuse (data-driven, real-time)
27-34. Signposting, rhetorical questions, sentence openers, reassurance kickers

### Writer Skill Patterns Checked
- Paragraph opener repetition (This/The/It trap)
- Rhythm monotony (same sentence length)
- Vague claims (lacking specific numbers/examples)
- List overuse (should be prose)
- Parallel structure breaks
- Transition word crutches
- Word economy issues
- Nominalization trap (using nouns instead of verbs)
